The strategy in one page

Your grades are perfect. What limits you is that your school system ran 11 years, while many countries count to 12. The plan is to apply where 11 years is accepted, fix the one weak score (IELTS Writing), and use a one-year bridge only for countries that demand 12.

IELTS Writing plan (target 6.5+)

  • Task 2 is two-thirds of the band. Answer every part of the question; that is the usual reason for 5.5.
  • Task 1 needs a clear overview sentence, or the band is capped at 5.
  • Fewer mistakes beat rare words. Aim for more than half your sentences error-free.
  • Two full timed writing tests a week with examiner-style feedback.
  • Computer-delivered IELTS allows a One Skill Retake of Writing within 60 days. Check that your target schools accept it.
  • Alternatives if Writing stalls: TOEFL iBT, Duolingo English Test, PTE Academic.
