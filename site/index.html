When a foundation is worth a year

  • It gives guaranteed progression to a named university (UQ College, Monash College, Trinity, Taylors, UTS College, Eynesbury, Holland ISC, NCUK, Canada's international colleges).
  • The country counts it as the missing 12th year: Australia, Italy, Malta, Malaysia, UK-model campuses in Dubai, Canadian pathway colleges.
  • In Germany it ends in the state exam (FH Aachen, CBS Prep4University). A private foundation opens only its own university.
  • It starts Jan–Mar 2027, so you finish your degree about as early as a direct Fall 2027 start.

When it is wasted

  • The country already admits you directly (US, Sweden, Finland, Hungary, Türkiye, UAE, Korea, Hong Kong, Baltics, Portugal).
  • Norway, Denmark want a year of university, not a foundation.
  • Erasmus Rotterdam, Tilburg reject foundation certificates. NTU/NUS want AP or A-levels.
  • Closed or unsuitable: Twente Pathway College (last intake Sept 2026), VU's VASVU (Dutch-taught, EU/resident only), Debrecen's foundation (asks 12 years; Debrecen admits you directly anyway).

Roadmap, Oct 2026 → Sep 2027

Two tracks run in parallel: direct applications for Fall 2027, and a foundation that can start in February 2027. Every date that matters is below; saved programmes add their own deadlines.

Track 1 · main

Direct entry, Fall 2027

15–20 applications where 11 years is accepted: US (Columbia GS), UAE (AUS, Khalifa), Türkiye (Koç, Sabancı, Bilkent, METU), Hong Kong (HKU, HKBU), Korea, Sweden, Finland, Hungary, Baltics, Portugal, Canada.

Unlocks: Writing 6.5 + SAT 1350+ by December.

Track 2 · parallel

Foundation from Feb 2027

UQ College (15 Feb), Monash College (22 Feb), Trinity Melbourne (Jan/Feb), UTS College, Eynesbury, or Holland ISC (Jan → Hanze), Heriot-Watt Dubai (Jan). These give guaranteed progression, and you finish about when a US degree started in Fall 2027 would.

Unlocks: new passport and an offer by mid-December.

Track 3 · safety

Factors that touch every application

Passport & IELTS expiry

Passport ends 21 Sep 2027, IELTS 2 Sep 2027. No multi-year visa is possible on the current passport.

Visa refusal risk

US: about 64% of Uzbek visitor visas refused (FY2024); global F-1 refusal 35% (2025). Canada approved only about 36% of new post-secondary permits (2025). Australia refusals 24% (Jun 2026). Schengen for Uzbek applicants about 16% (Germany about 20%, Latvia about 10%).

Recognition back home

Uzbekistan recognises degrees from universities in the global top 1,000 (QS, THE, ARWU), and from foreign branches in Uzbekistan, without nostrification. Liberal arts colleges, applied-sciences universities and colleges usually need nostrification.

Military service

Full-time students get a deferral. For study abroad, file a notarised translation of your enrolment certificate with your district military commissariat.

The 5-year gap

Write a month-by-month account of 2022–2026 with proof. Visa officers (Australia's Genuine Student test) and essays will ask.

Old-system confusion

Many foreign "Uzbekistan" pages describe the pre-2017 12-year lyceum system. Always say your Attestat is from the 11-year general school.
