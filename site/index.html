Scores arrive about 13 days after the test. Each card shows which of your Tier 1 and 2 deadlines that sitting still reaches. Pick one and your checklist follows.

Passport and IELTS, in this order

  1. Passport first (suggested by 31 Oct). A biometric passport for travel abroad takes about 10 working days.
  2. Then book the IELTS with the new passport. The test centre checks the same ID you registered with. Computer-delivered results arrive in 1–5 days.
  3. Sit it by late November. If Writing stays below 6.5, the One Skill Retake lets you redo only Writing within 60 days, still before the January deadlines.
  4. Your current IELTS (7.5) stays valid until 2 Sep 2027, so nothing breaks while you wait.

Things to keep in mind

  • Visa odds. US visitor-visa refusals for Uzbek citizens ran about 64% (FY2024) and global F-1 refusals about 35% (2025). Canada approved only about 36% of new post-secondary study permits in 2025. Schengen refusals for Uzbek applicants were about 16%.
  • Recognition at home. Degrees from universities in the global top 1,000 (QS, THE, ARWU) are recognised in Uzbekistan without nostrification. Rows show a ranking band.
  • Military service. Full-time students get a deferment; file your enrolment letter with the district commissariat after you accept an offer.
  • The 2022–2026 gap. Keep a month-by-month account with proof for essays, interviews and visas.
  • Recency rules. Khalifa University, KFUPM, Alfaisal, NYU Abu Dhabi, Sciences Po and others only take recent school leavers; they are marked closed.
