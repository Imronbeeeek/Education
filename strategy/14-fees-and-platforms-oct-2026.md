# 14 · Application fees, free routes and one-form platforms (9 Oct 2026)

Every university on the site now shows its application fee under its dates, plus a "Pay $0" line when there is a way to avoid it. The Programmes filters have a new **Free or fee waiver** switch. Strategy has a fees panel, and the Checklist tracks each application's status (Not started → Submitted → Offer).

## The cheapest way to apply

1. **One form for many universities.**
   - **Common App:** the US, Qatar's Education City, NYU Shanghai, Duke Kunshan.
   - **OUAC:** every Ontario university, CAD 156 for 3 choices, then CAD 50 each.
   - **universityadmissions.se (Sweden):** SEK 900 per semester, covers every choice.
   - **Studyinfo (Finland):** €100 once per term, covers every choice, no waivers.
   - **optagelse.dk (Denmark):** free.
   - **Study in Saudi:** free.
   - **uni-assist (Germany):** €75, then €30 for each further university in the same semester; some universities pay it for you.
2. **Free routes.**
   - Georgetown-Qatar waives the fee for every completed application.
   - Duke Kunshan charges no fee.
   - Stipendium Hungaricum: the application and entrance exams are free.
   - GKS (Korea) and Study in Saudi are free.
   - Denmark, Norway and most of Austria charge nothing.
   - Many Turkish private universities charge nothing; Sabancı charges $30.
   - US colleges with no fee (2026 lists): Tulane, Macalester, Grinnell, Carleton, St. Olaf, Colorado College, Dayton.
3. **The Common App fee waiver.**
   - Answer the fee-waiver questions in your Common App profile; a counselor or official at your school confirms them.
   - Each college decides whether to accept it. One that refuses asks for the fee after you submit.
   - Georgetown (Washington) accepts waiver requests from international applicants.
   - CMU-Qatar ($70) takes the Common App waiver.
   - Many colleges also waive on an honest email request.
4. **Fees with no way around them:**
   - Finland €100 and Sweden SEK 900.
   - HKU HK$780 (up from HK$600) and UBC CAD 173.25.
   - OUAC CAD 156.
   - The UC application (≈ $95 per campus for international applicants; confirm).
   - Vilnius University €100 (up to 3 programmes) and AUS AED 400 + VAT.

With the 17 programmes saved by default, fees come to about $1,250, and about $750 of that can be brought to $0.

## Design changes in this pass

Ideas taken from Common App's dashboard and Niche's tiered college lists:
- **Application tracker:** the list of saved universities now works like Common App's "My Colleges", with a status for each application.
- **Removed clutter:** the tier selector inside every country header is gone. Tiers are still changed in Countries & systems.

## Design changes (third pass)

Following the search-and-filter pattern used by college-search and shopping sites (one search bar, a Filters button with a count, results first):
- **One search bar** with a **Filters** button that shows how many filters are on, plus sort. Show, chance, the "only show" switches, "what if my scores change" and the deadline key all moved into the Filters panel.
- **Removed:** the intro box, the colour legend, the page header's eyebrow line, the tags on each row and the field list. Each row now shows its name, place, recognition, dates, fee, status and chance.
- **Guide:** Courses and Glossary are now one "Guide" tab.
- **On phones:** country shortcuts sit on one swipeable row, and country descriptions show three lines (tap for the rest).
