### How the chance bands work

| Band | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Long shot | Under ~10% admitted. Even perfect applicants are usually refused. |
| Reach | ~10–25% admitted. |
| Target | ~25–55% admitted, or criteria-based admission where the competition is real. |
| Likely | Over ~55% admitted, or admission on published criteria you already meet. |

- Bands come from published or widely reported admission rates. Admission rates for international students are often lower than the overall rate.
- Tier 1 bands were set one by one. Tier 2–3 entries are estimated from their ranking and marked "Estimated from ranking".
- A band describes competition, not eligibility. Eligibility stays in the status chip: Open now, Needs a score, Bridge year first, or Closed.

## USA: the analysis

### 1. Routes that fit a 2022 graduate

| Route | Rule | For you |
|---|---|---|
| Standard first-year (Common App) | No university enrolment since school | ✅ Fall 2027. If you took any university courses, you must list them, and you may become a transfer applicant. |
| Columbia School of General Studies | Break of a year or more in education | ✅ Early action 1 Mar 2027 (advised for F-1 students). IELTS 7.0 / Duolingo 120 / TOEFL 100. |
| **Yale Eli Whitney Students Program** (new) | 5+ years since school | ✅ You qualify for Fall 2027. Transfer Common App, ~1 Mar 2027. International students welcome; F-1 students must study full time. Tiny intake. |
| Brown RUE (new, closed) | 6+ years since school | ❌ Opens to you only for Fall 2028 (~1 Mar 2028). |
| Minerva University (new) | Own process; no SAT, no letters | ✅ EA 1 Nov · RD 5 Jan · 2 Mar 2027. About 1–3% admitted, and unranked, so nostrification would be needed. |

### 2. English: the Writing 5.5 problem is real in the USA

| University | Rule | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Michigan | IELTS 7.0 and **6.5 in every section**; Duolingo not accepted | Blocked until Writing reaches 6.5 |
| UIUC, Purdue | 6.5 overall, 6.0 per section | Blocked until Writing reaches 6.0 |
| Columbia College | TOEFL 105 minimum | Needs a very strong retake |
| Boston University | 7.0+ with consistent sections; One Skill Retake not accepted | Full retake |
| NYU, Northeastern | No minimum / competitive 7.5–8.0 | Overall fine; Writing is a visible weakness |
| UC campuses, USC, UMass Amherst | One Skill Retake not accepted | Retake the full test |
| Penn State, Ohio State | 6.5 overall | ✅ |
| Arizona State | 6.0 overall | ✅ |
| George Mason, Rowan | Accept the One Skill Retake | A Writing-only retake works |

**Decision:** sit one **full** IELTS in November aiming for Writing 6.5–7.0. Duolingo (125+) is the fallback for most universities, but not Michigan.

### 3. SAT/ACT policies for Fall 2027

- **Required:**
  - Ivy and peer universities: Harvard, Yale, Stanford, MIT, Caltech (scores by 31 Dec), Penn, Cornell, Brown, Dartmouth, Johns Hopkins, Georgetown and Carnegie Mellon.
  - Public universities: Purdue, Georgia Tech, UT Austin, Florida's public universities (SAT, ACT or CLT), the University of Georgia, Georgia State, Tennessee and Auburn.
  - Reported as required, but confirm: Texas A&M and UNC Chapel Hill.
- **Optional:**
  - Princeton and Columbia, for this year only.
  - NYU, USC, Boston University, Northeastern, UChicago, Michigan, Wisconsin, Maryland, Ohio State, Penn State, Rutgers, Rochester, Case Western, Arizona State and most mid-tier universities.
  - Alabama asks only when the GPA is below 3.0.
- **Not used:** University of California, California State University and Minerva.

### 6. Visa facts

- **Entry restrictions:** Uzbekistan is **not** on the 39-country list under Proclamation 10998 (in force since 1 Jan 2026). Check again before paying a deposit.
- **Tashkent embassy:** the F-1 interview wait was about **6 weeks** in Sep 2026.
- **Social media:** since June 2025, F-1 applicants must set their social-media accounts to public.

## The other Tier 1 countries: new facts

| Country | Finding | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Canada | UBC assesses grades 11–12 or equivalent. McGill's attestat list names Russia, Kazakhstan, Armenia and Georgia but not Uzbekistan. Toronto and Waterloo publish no Uzbek entry. | Email Toronto, McGill and Waterloo about the Attestat. Alberta remains the clearest fit. |
| Hong Kong | HKU has no Uzbek listing and assesses unlisted qualifications case by case. EdUHK and HKUST(GZ) list the Uzbek Attestat. | Ask HKU directly; the SAT strengthens every Hong Kong application. |
| China | **XJTU English-taught engineering** (new row): CSCA required, IELTS 6.0, three windows (Nov–Dec, Jan–Apr, May–Jun in the 2026 pattern), and first-year scholarships up to full tuition. | A realistic target once the CSCA is done. |
| Türkiye | Koç: merit scholarships of 25–100% come automatically with the offer; SAT scores older than 2 years are not accepted. **Bahçeşehir** (new row): spring intake starts 18 Jan 2027. | Türkiye is your safest Tier 1 country. |
| France | **SKEMA** states a 12-year schooling rule (new row, marked "confirm"). | Confirm with every French BBA before paying a fee. |
| Saudi Arabia | **Prince Sultan University** admits only Saudi or GCC nationals and legal residents. | Stays closed (with Alfaisal). |
| Qatar | **UDST** (new row): the fall cycle opened in February in 2026; eligibility for applicants from abroad is unclear. | Low priority; ask first. |
