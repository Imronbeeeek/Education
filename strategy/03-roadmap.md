# 3. Actionable Education Roadmap (2 Oct 2026 → Sept 2027, with a 2028 fallback)

Two tracks run in parallel:
- **Track P (Primary):** Fall 2027 direct entry, portfolio across 8–10 countries.
- **Track S (Secondary):** Spring 2027 start, in 2–3 countries with short visa lead times.

Dates marked ✅ were confirmed during research (Oct 2026). Others are typical-cycle estimates; verify on each portal.

## Plan B: 2028 entry (if Fall 2027 results are unsatisfactory)

1. **Bridge year 2027/28**, chosen by target system:
   - **Germany:** FH Aachen Freshman Program (English), CBS Prep4University (English), Constructor Foundation Year (TestAS ≥ 190)
   - **Italy:** Pavia, Ca' Foscari, or Perugia/Siena Stranieri Foundation
   - **Netherlands WO:** Holland ISC
   - **Australia Go8:** Trinity/UNSW College/Monash College, already possible from **Feb/Mar 2027**
   - **Singapore NTU/NUS:** **3 AP exams (May 2027)** + SAT ≥ 1250
2. **Or** complete **Year 1 at a Category-A university** (e.g. Hungary, Türkiye, Baltics, Korea) and apply in **2028 as S2**. That unlocks Germany, Italy, Norway, Denmark, NL WO and Australia directly, and makes you a **US transfer** applicant.
3. **Sit the Uzbek national test in summer 2027** if you want the Austria / KU Leuven / VILNIUS TECH routes for 2028.
