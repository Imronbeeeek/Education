# 07 · Additional factors (research round, Oct 2026)

These findings were added in the third research round. They are reflected in `site/index.html` (Universities → Country rules, Roadmap → "Factors that touch every application", Checklist).

## Recognition of your degree back in Uzbekistan
- Under Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 620 (24 Jul 2019), Uzbekistan recognises diplomas **without nostrification** when they come from:
  - universities ranked in the **global top 1,000** of QS, THE or ARWU; or
  - foreign branches and joint programmes operating in Uzbekistan.
- Uzbekistan ratified the UNESCO Global Convention on recognition in Nov 2025.
- **Consequence:**
  - Liberal arts colleges, most applied-sciences universities (HBO, Finnish UAS, German FH), French business schools and community colleges are usually unranked, so their degrees need nostrification.
  - The site tags every entry "UZ-recognised" or "Nostrification" and lets you filter by it.

## Military service
- Full-time students are deferred for the length of study.
- For study abroad, file a notarised translation of the enrolment certificate with your district military commissariat. This is added as a checklist task.
