**Target:** IELTS Academic **7.5–8.0 overall with Writing ≥ 6.5** (stretch 7.0).

**Writing-specific plan:**
- **Task 2 (66% of the Writing band):** a 4-paragraph structure (clear position → two developed body paragraphs with examples → conclusion). 260–290 words. Every paragraph must answer *all* parts of the prompt (Task Response is the usual cause of 5.5).
- **Task 1 (Academic graphs/processes):** an overview sentence is mandatory (otherwise Task Achievement is capped at 5). Group data; don't list every number.
- **Lexical/grammar:** fewer errors beats rarer words. Mix complex sentences, but keep error-free sentences above 50%.
- **Practice:** 2 full timed Writing tests per week with examiner-style feedback (British Council / IDP / a certified tutor). Practise on a computer if taking computer-delivered IELTS.
- **Safety net:** computer-delivered IELTS → **One Skill Retake (Writing)** within 60 days if Writing alone underperforms. Confirm acceptance by your top targets.

**Alternatives** (if IELTS Writing stalls):
- **TOEFL iBT:** new adaptive format since Jan 2026, reported on a 1–6 scale alongside 0–120 during the transition. Accepted by the US, Canada, Europe and Asia.
- **Duolingo English Test:** US + many others.
- **PTE Academic:** Australia, the UK model, many others.

Check each institution's accepted list and sub-score rules.
