## 5.2 Legalisation by destination

Uzbekistan is a **Hague Apostille Convention** party. Destination status as of 2026:

| Destination | Mechanism | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| All EU/EEA targets (AT, BE, DK, FI, FR, DE, HU, IS, IT, LV, LT, LU, MT, NL, NO, PT, ES, SE) | **Apostille** | VILNIUS TECH guidance names Uzbekistan's national testing/assessment agency as the apostille authority for education documents. Confirm the current authority |
| United States, Canada (Apostille since 2024), Australia | Apostille (often not even requested for admission) | US: possibly a WES/ECE evaluation at some publics |
| South Korea, China (Apostille since Nov 2023), Hong Kong | **Apostille** | Korea may also require embassy confirmation or online verification |
| Türkiye | **Apostille** + notarised Turkish translation | Then **MEB Denklik** (e-Denklik) |
| Saudi Arabia (since 2022), Bahrain | **Apostille** | Then Ministry equivalence |
| **UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Jordan, Malaysia** | **Consular legalisation** (not Apostille parties) | Uzbek MFA → destination embassy (Tashkent or accredited) → destination MFA on arrival. Allow 3–6 weeks |

## 5.5 Name consistency

Use the passport spelling **KHAYDAROV IMRONBEK** everywhere. The Attestat shows *XAYDAROV IMRONBEK DONIYORJON O'G'LI* (Uzbek Latin) and *ХАЙДАРОВ ИМРОНБЕК* (Cyrillic). Ask sworn translators to render the name exactly as in the passport, and add the patronymic "DONIYORJON UGLI" where forms have a middle-name field.
