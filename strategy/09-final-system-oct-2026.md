# 09 · Final system: universities, systems, courses (8 Oct 2026)

This is the last research pass behind the finished website (`site/index.html`, [live page](https://claude.ai/artifact/9GGSnY86Wd3Dy6C3iCUVTz)).

## What the website now contains

| Area | Content |
|---|---|
| Universities → Programmes | 227 degree entries and 41 foundation routes. Rows also list another ~570 institutions that follow the same rule, about 800 in all. Each entry shows city, ranking band, fields of study, courses, status for you, what you still need, application window, suggested date, final deadline and official website. Tier 1 universities are now listed individually. Filters cover field, ranking/recognition, spring start and saved; you can sort and compare up to 3 side by side. |
| Universities → Countries & systems | All 33 countries, as cards or a comparison table. Each covers: degree length, institution types, language, how to apply, intakes, admission basis, rule for an 11-year Attestat, foundation, approximate cost (information only), work rights after graduating, visa and spring options. |
| Universities → Courses guide | 10 fields of study. For each: what you study, what admissions look for, fit with your grades, length, strongest options, outcomes, and the best open matches in the selected tier. |
| Universities → Glossary | 52 terms (homologation, PAL, CSCA, Universitaly, OPT, IANG…). Terms are also tappable wherever they appear on the site. |
| Roadmap | Action-window chart, SAT sitting planner, graduation timeline and a month-by-month list. Download for Google Calendar (CSV). |
| Checklist | Tasks with suggested and final dates; filters (all / main goal / tests & papers / applications); completed list with undo; custom tasks; shortlist; CSV downloads; start over. |

## New entries worth knowing

- **Universidad de Navarra (Spain)**
  - Private; ranked in the QS top 500.
  - Medicine taught partly in English, plus international nursing, economics, communication and an international science programme.
  - Foreign diplomas need UNED accreditation, and some degrees add an on-campus exam ([UNAV degrees](https://en.unav.edu/studies/degrees)).
- **Les Roches Marbella (Spain)**
  - BSc Global Hospitality Management; IELTS 5.5 accepted.
  - Starts September and February ([Les Roches](https://lesroches.edu/programs/bachelor-science-hospitality-management/)).
- **PolyU School of Hotel & Tourism Management (Hong Kong)**: ranked world no. 1 in its field for ten years running ([PolyU SHTM](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/shtm/About-SHTM/World-class-Hotel-and-Tourism-School?sc_lang=en)).
- **Sorbonne University Abu Dhabi**: French B2 required, except for physics ([Sorbonne AD admissions](https://www.sorbonne.ae/admissions)).
- **Ranking bands**:
  - QS 2026 places KFUPM 67th, King Saud 143rd and King Abdulaziz 163rd.
  - The other bands on the site are approximate QS bands.
  - A top-1,000 place (QS, THE or ARWU) means recognition in Uzbekistan without nostrification ([QS 2026](https://www.qs.com/insights/qs-world-university-rankings-2026)).

## How to use the finished system

1. **Universities**
   - Choose a tier, then save 8–12 Tier 1 programmes and 3–5 Tier 2 programmes.
   - Use the Courses guide if you are unsure about a field.
2. **Roadmap**
   - Pick your SAT sitting.
   - Download the calendar CSV and import it into Google Calendar (Settings › Import & export).
3. **Checklist**
   - Work from "Up next". Done tasks move to Completed.
   - If a programme's status changes after your IELTS or SAT, update "What if my scores change?" in Universities.
