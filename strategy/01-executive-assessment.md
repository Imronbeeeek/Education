## 1.2 How the 11-year Uzbek Attestat is processed across the 33 countries

Countries fall into **five recognition families**:

### Family I: "Access-in-home-country" principle → **direct entry possible (Category A)**
The certificate is accepted if it gives university access in Uzbekistan. Year-counting is not applied.

| Country | Confirmation |
|---|---|
| **Sweden** | universityadmissions.se lists the Shahodatnoma as meeting general entry requirements |
| **Finland** | Eligibility = eligibility in the issuing country |
| **Hungary** | Corvinus: "11 years acceptable where this is the national standard" |
| **Latvia** | LU country list names the Uzbek attestat |
| **Lithuania** | VU principle; VILNIUS TECH adds the national exam |
| **Portugal** | International Student Statute |
| **Belgium – Flanders** | KU Leuven / UGent individual assessment |
| **France** | DAP principle; Études en France |
| **Türkiye** | YÖK + MEB denklik processes 11-year systems; admission on diploma grade |
| **UAE** | The equivalency regulation explicitly accepts the CIS **11-year** certificate |
| **South Korea** | MOE recognises full schooling in 11-grade countries, Uzbekistan named |
| **China** | No year rule; age windows instead |
| **Hong Kong** | HKBU lists the Uzbek Attestat; HKU individual merit |
| **United States** | Holistic; no year rule |
| **Canada** | Most provinces/universities; Alberta lists the Uzbek certificate |

### Family II: Home-access proof **plus the national entrance test** → **A with the DTM/BMBA certificate, otherwise B/C**
- **Austria**: special university entrance qualification.
- **Belgium – KU Leuven**.
- **Lithuania – VILNIUS TECH**.
- **Hong Kong – CUHK/PolyU** (by analogy with EGE/UNT).

## 1.3 Bottom line (headline recommendations)

1. **Fall 2027 is highly achievable with direct entry** in **17 countries** (18 counting Qatar's Education City):
   - **Europe:** Sweden, Finland, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Portugal, Belgium-Flanders (with proof), Spain (private universities), France (private schools), Netherlands (HBO)
   - **Asia & Middle East:** Türkiye, UAE, South Korea, China, Hong Kong
   - **Americas:** US, Canada
2. **The highest-leverage actions this month:**
   - **(a)** Renew the passport.
   - **(b)** Book an **IELTS retake** targeting **Writing ≥ 6.5**. Use the computer-delivered test so you can do a *One Skill Retake* of Writing within 60 days if needed.
   - **(c)** Register for the **7 Nov 2026 SAT** (deadline **23 Oct**), and the 5 Dec 2026 sitting as backup.
   - **(d)** Retrieve or confirm your **national entrance-test certificate**.
   - **(e)** Start **apostille / legalisation**. Note that the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Jordan and Malaysia are **not** Apostille parties and need consular legalisation.
3. **Spring 2027 is realistic in five places only** because of visa lead times:
   - **UAE** (January intakes)
   - **Malaysia** (UUM direct, or Foundation, Feb/Mar)
   - **Australia** (Foundation, Feb/Mar)
   - **Türkiye** (foundation universities' spring semester)
   - **South Korea** (mid-tier universities' March admissions still open, or a Korean language institute)

   Also check **February HBO intakes in the Netherlands** and **Baltic February intakes** this week.
4. **Do not spend 2027 fees** on Sciences Po, NYU Abu Dhabi, Qatar University, the University of Bahrain, Kuwait University scholarships, Jordanian universities, Uni.lu, Türkiye Bursları, Texas A&M Qatar or women-only institutions (Korea's Ewha/Sookmyung, Saudi Arabia's PNU/Effat, Bahrain's RUW, Kuwait's Box Hill). The rules exclude you.
5. **If you prefer Germany, Italy, the Netherlands' research universities, Denmark, Norway or the Australian Go8**, the cleanest plan is a **one-year bridge in 2027/28**:
   - German: an English-track Studienkolleg (FH Aachen Freshman Program, CBS Prep4University) or Constructor University's Foundation Year
   - Italian: a Foundation Year (Pavia, Ca' Foscari, Perugia/Siena Stranieri)
   - Dutch / Australian: Holland ISC, Trinity, UNSW College or Monash College
   - **or** one year at a good English-medium university in a Category A country, which then counts as the "+1 year" everywhere.
