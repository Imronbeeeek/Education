## South Korea in detail

**How they read an 11-year Attestat.** The Ministry of Education's baseline is "more than 12 years of school", and it leaves admission rules to each university. Earlier notes said Korea recognises 11-year systems outright; that was too strong.
- **Accept completed secondary school** (reported by secondary guides): KAIST, Yonsei and GKS.
- **Explicitly states 12 years:** KOREATECH.
- **What to do:** ask each university in writing before paying a fee. Your apostilled Attestat and transcript are the documents.

**The 55 entries, by section:**
- **Top Seoul (9):** SNU, Yonsei UIC, Korea University, SKKU, Hanyang, Sogang, Kyung Hee, Chung-Ang, HUFS.
- **Science and technology institutes (6):** KAIST, POSTECH, UNIST, GIST, DGIST, KOREATECH. All teach in English.
- **Other Seoul and capital area (16):** e.g. University of Seoul, Konkuk, Dongguk, Hongik, Inha, Ajou, Gachon.
- **National universities (10):** e.g. Pusan, Kyungpook, Chonnam, Chungnam.
- **Regional and international-focused (12):** e.g. SolBridge, Woosong Endicott, Handong, Sun Moon, Keimyung Adams College.
- **Routes (2):** GKS-U, plus women's and military universities (closed to you).
- **"More" row:** names 28 further universities.
- **Language route:** the Yonsei Korean Language Institute (D-4) is added next to Korea University's KLC.

**Language.** English tracks need only your IELTS. Most other programmes expect TOPIK 3–4, which is why 36 entries show "Needs a score or document".

**Visa.**
- **IEQAS restrictions:** universities that fail IEQAS cannot issue D-2 or D-4 visas for a year. Twenty were restricted from September 2026; the ten names published in the reports found are small institutions not on your list. Check the list before applying.
- **TOPIK 4 for Uzbek applicants:** the earlier note said non-certified universities require it. This could not be confirmed, so it is now marked as a question for each university.

## Latvia and Lithuania

- **Latvia:**
  - **Deadlines** are set by citizenship. The University of Latvia's 2026 dates: 1 May for "additional assessment" countries, 21 Jun for visa-requiring non-EU citizens. RTU used 1 May, 15 Jun or 15 Jul depending on the country group.
  - **Plan for 1 May 2027.**
  - **Admission tests:** RTU adds an online maths test, and SSE Riga runs its own maths, logic and English test in April.
  - **Recognition:** RTU and LU are in the top 1,000, so their degrees are recognised in Uzbekistan without nostrification. Private institutions are not ranked.
- **Lithuania:**
  - **Vilnius University:** non-EU deadline 1 May (2026 pattern); applications open around 1 December.
  - **KTU:** about 1 June, with 50+ English programmes.
  - **ISM and LCC:** around 30 April and 15 April – 15 May.
  - **VILNIUS TECH:** stays closed because it asks for Uzbek national entrance-exam results.

## Malta, Iceland, Luxembourg

- **Malta:**
  - **University of Malta:** compares foreign certificates with its Matriculation Certificate, so you start in Foundation Studies (IELTS 5.5 with 5.0 bands, which you meet). The non-EU visa deadline was 1 July 2026, 14:00, and later applications roll to the next year.
  - **American University of Malta:** its published rules use Maltese MATSEC or A-levels, so ask how it treats your certificate.
  - **Queen Mary's Gozo medical campus:** closed (UK medical entry).
- **Iceland:**
  - **Equivalence:** both universities want the equivalent of the 13-year stúdentspróf.
  - **Deadlines:** non-EU students start in autumn only. Reykjavík University closes 31 January and the University of Iceland 1 February 2027.
  - **Fees:** the University of Iceland may charge non-EEA tuition from autumn 2027 (unconfirmed).
- **Luxembourg:**
  - **University of Luxembourg:** the Ministry's equivalence still requires 12 consecutive years. It takes at least six weeks and costs EUR 125 for non-convention countries, and must be in hand before you apply. Last year's non-EU window was 1 Feb – 24 Mar 2026.
  - **Private institutions:** LUNEX, EBU and UBI remain, with recognition to check.
