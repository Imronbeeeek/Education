### Notes per country

**China**
- New CSCA entrance test: maths, plus physics or chemistry, taken in English.
- Required at universities in the Chinese Government Scholarship scheme, including Fudan's English-taught programmes and CSC scholarships.
- 2027 sessions: 23–24 Jan, 13–14 Mar, 24–25 Apr and 26–27 Jun.
- Tsinghua requires HSK 5; PKU uses its own entrance test in Chinese.

**Italy**
- The MUR circular requires a B1 Italian certificate for a foundation-course visa.
- Pavia's 2027/28 window 1 closes 15 Oct 2026; later windows follow.
- Cattolica, Ca' Foscari, UNINT and Pisa also run foundation years for applicants with 11 years of school.

**Saudi Arabia**
- Study in Saudi scholarships require:
  - ages 17–25;
  - a certificate obtained within the last 5 years;
  - authentication by the Saudi mission.
- IUM is for Muslim men and asks for 12 years of school or equivalent.
- Alfaisal admits graduates of the last 2 years only; 5+ years needs University Council approval.
- Alfaisal also needs 12 years of school plus Saudi Ministry equivalency.
