# 15 · Site reorganised, medicine removed, more fees (9 Oct 2026)

## What changed and why

Before this update the website stacked four layers of navigation before any content: three section tabs, then tier lanes, then four sub-tabs, then search, field chips and country chips. The facts strip and a "Next" banner filled the first screen of every section.

The new layout follows common patterns from college-search and tracker tools:

- **3–5 equal destinations** in a bottom bar on phones and a top bar on desktop (Material Design's bottom-navigation guidance).
- **Main filters up front, the rest behind one "Filters" button**, with active filters counted (Scoir's college-search refresh; Edinburgh's course-finder testing).
- **A "My Colleges"-style list** with each school's status and deadlines (Common App dashboard).
- **A home dashboard** with next tasks, deadlines and the list summary (application-tracker templates).

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Today's date, your facts (Attestat, IELTS, SAT, DTM, passport, age), the next 3 tasks with a Done button, your next 5 deadlines from saved universities (key dates if nothing is saved), a My-list summary (saved / preparing / submitted / fees, chance balance), your 5 best countries from the scorecard, and "Explore by field" shortcuts |
| **Universities** | **Browse** (search, tier, field, Filters; country chips on one line), **Countries** (one page per country: strategy, chances, risks, timeline, suggested shortlist and how the national system works; the scorecard and the systems comparison table fold underneath), **Guide** (course guide and glossary) |
| **My list** | Every saved programme with its chance, final deadline and days left, fee (and whether it can be $0), what is still needed (e.g. "SAT 1300+"), the university's website and an application-status selector; sort by deadline or country; then the balance check and the fees guide |
| **Plan** | **Tasks** (progress, up next, grouped tasks, add your own, completed) and **Timeline** (action windows, SAT planner, passport-then-IELTS order, graduation chart, month-by-month calendar) |

Visual changes:

- a slim sticky top bar with the four places;
- one body font, with the monospace labels gone;
- normal-width headings;
- softer borders and lighter card shadows;
- a subtle background glow;
- the selected tier is shown as a filled pill.

On phones the bottom bar has four icons with labels and count badges ("★ 17" saved, "38" tasks).

## Medicine removed

The medicine & health field (medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, nursing, health sciences) is gone from the field chips, the course guide and every entry.

**Removed: 13 entries that only taught medicine.**

- Weill Cornell Medicine-Qatar
- University of Calgary in Qatar (nursing)
- Semmelweis University
- University of Pécs medical preparatory course
- Tung Wah College
- English-taught MBBS in China
- İstinye, Acıbadem and Biruni universities
- Humanitas and Vita-Salute San Raffaele
- RCSI Bahrain
- Lithuanian University of Health Sciences

**Kept: 207 universities that also teach your fields.** For these, only the medicine tag and the medical courses in their descriptions were removed. Examples are UPenn, Johns Hopkins, Toronto, HKU, Koç, Bologna, Padua, Monash, Vilnius University and many Turkish and Spanish public universities.

**Other adjustments:**

- China's "Medicine (MBBS)" section was merged into "More universities".
- The Hungary strategy no longer mentions Semmelweis or medical entrance exams.
- Country degree lengths no longer quote medicine's 6 years.

The site now lists **1,005 universities and 41 foundation routes in 32 countries**.

## New application fees

"Official" means the university's or system's own page. "Secondary" means an aggregator or an older official page; confirm these before paying.

| UW–Madison | $80 | Common App waiver | Official 2026 UW application form |
| University of Michigan | $75 | Common App waiver | Secondary (Big Ten guide) |
| UIUC | $75 international ($50 domestic) | Common App waiver | Secondary |

| Illinois Tech | ≈ $75 | Common App waiver | Secondary |
