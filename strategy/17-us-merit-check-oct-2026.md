## 3. Biggest confirmed awards

**Full-ride or full-tuition awards open to international students** (all competitive):
- SMU President's Scholars: full tuition and fees plus a housing award.
- University of Houston Tier One: full tuition and fees plus two years of housing and meals; apply by 1 Nov.
- Marquette Global Scholar: full tuition, housing, food and fees for up to 2 international students; apply by 1 Mar.
- Lamar University Presidential: full ride for SAT 1330+ (limited number); priority 15 Jan.
- SIU Edwardsville Meridian: full tuition, fees, room and board.
- Clarkson Ignite Presidential (10 a year) and Coe Global Leadership (1 a year): full tuition.

**Large automatic or near-automatic awards at private colleges:**
- Willamette: $52,000 a year for every international applicant.
- Xavier (Ohio) $23,000–30,000 a year and Puget Sound $18,000–33,000 a year, both automatic for international applicants.
- Cornell College $35,000–40,000; Coe up to $42,000; Clarkson up to $40,000; Washington College $33,000–38,000; Ohio Wesleyan $37,000; Millsaps $21,000–34,000; Ursinus $30,000–40,000 (reported); Eckerd up to $25,000.
- These colleges list a full cost of roughly $55,000–85,000 a year, so even a large award leaves $20,000–45,000 a year to pay.

**Public universities with automatic or large awards:**
- Missouri $10,000 or $20,000; Missouri S&T $15,000; Idaho nearly $16,000; Middle Tennessee $6,000–16,000; Rowan $1,000–19,000; North Florida $6,000–14,000; Towson $6,000–12,000; Northern Arizona up to $11,000; Western Washington and SUNY Geneseo up to $10,000; Grand Valley about $6,000 for every international admit.
- **Texas rule worth knowing:** a competitive scholarship of $1,000 or more lets an international student pay the in-state tuition rate at a Texas public university (East Texas A&M says so on its own page).

## 4. What this means for you

1. **The SAT decides many of these awards.** Lamar, Middle Tennessee, UNC Wilmington and Coe tie their tiers to SAT scores. Your Attestat 5.0 meets most GPA tiers. A strong SAT in November or December 2026 opens the top tiers.
2. **Apply early.** Several awards are first-come or have priority dates before the main deadline: Houston 1 Nov, Towson 15 Nov, Memphis 1 Dec, SUNY Geneseo 1 Jan, North Florida and Lamar 15 Jan.
3. **Most awards are automatic.** Your admission application is your scholarship application at most of these universities. The exceptions want a separate form or essay: Marquette Global Scholar, Coe Global Leadership and Northern Iowa Excel.
4. **Treat "not confirmed" as a question, not a no.** Email the admissions office and ask: "Do international first-year applicants qualify for merit scholarships, and how much?"

University websites could not be opened directly from this environment, so a source is listed as the university's own page only when a search returned that page's text.
- The site shows the evidence level on every US row: checked on the university's page, secondary source, or well-known policy.
- Amounts change every year. Confirm on the linked page before planning around a figure.
