## 3. Funding from Uzbekistan: El-Yurt Umidi

- **2026/27 round:** 500 places, 167 of them for bachelor's degrees, at universities in the QS or THE top 300 (or on its approved list). Applications ran 3–24 April 2026.
- **Coverage:** tuition, visa, insurance, flights, rent and living costs.
- **Reported conditions:**
  - age 30 or under;
  - IELTS 6.0 or higher (an unconditional offer may replace it);
  - an admission letter;
  - five years of work in Uzbekistan after the degree.
- **Automatic award (2025 decree):** anyone admitted to a top-100 university with full tuition covered gets the scholarship without competing.

**What this means for you.** A full-tuition merit award from a top-100 US university can be completed by El-Yurt Umidi. The 2027 round should open around April, after US decisions arrive. Each US row now shows whether the university is likely in the top 100 or top 300. Confirm the exact rank.

## 4. Visa: what applies to you in 2026–27

**Rules in force**
- **Travel ban:** Uzbekistan is **not** restricted. Proclamation 10998 has applied since 1 Jan 2026, with 19 countries fully restricted and 19 partly (student visas included). Turkmenistan's student-visa restriction was lifted.
- **Interview:** must be in your country of nationality or residence (State Department, Sept–Oct 2025). Tashkent is the only post; the wait was about 1.5 months in Sept 2026.
- **Social media:** list every account used in the last five years on the DS-160. Student applicants are told to make them public.
- **Fees:**
  - SEVIS $350;
  - MRV $185;
  - $250 visa integrity fee, collected at issuance at some embassies since about May 2026.

**Rules that may change**
- **Length of stay:** the fixed-period rule, published 17 Jul 2026, was **blocked by a federal court on 14 Sep 2026**. Duration of status continues for now.
- **Work after graduation:** OPT still exists, but the government has said it wants to restrict it.

**Refusal data**
- 35% of F-1 applications worldwide were refused in 2025, and 41% in Asia.
- There is no official F-1 figure for Uzbekistan. Uzbek visitor visas were refused 64% of the time in FY2024, so expect close questions on funding and ties.
- 1,310 Uzbek students were in the US in 2024/25, 787 of them undergraduates.

**What makes the file strong, and which universities help**
- A scholarship letter covering most costs.
- A well-known university, and a major that follows from your record and your 2022–2026 years.
- Ties to Uzbekistan, including the El-Yurt Umidi return obligation.
- A consistent story across the DS-160, essays and interview.

Each US row shows a visa-file reading:
- **Strong:** a top-100 university, or one that meets full need or offers free tuition.
- **Fair:** a ranked university, or one with merit aid.
- **Prepare well:** a less-known university.

This reading is our judgement, not an official rating.

## 5. How to stand out

1. Present 2022–2026 as experience, with hours and results.
2. Raise IELTS Writing to 6.5–7.0, or score 125+ on Duolingo.
3. Score SAT 1450+ for most merit awards, or 1500+ for the most selective universities.
4. Use your background: few Uzbek applicants, three languages, and a plan for home.
5. Show one deep interest with something concrete.
6. Get letters from 2022 teachers plus a current supervisor.
7. Write "Why us" essays that name specific courses and people.
8. Take every interview offered, and show interest at smaller colleges.
9. Apply early where the money is. Merit deadlines fall from 1 Nov to 1 Dec, so consider the 7 Nov SAT.

Each US row also says **what that university looks for**:
- written notes for about 60 universities, from MIT's hands-on making to Columbia GS's non-traditional path and Berea's financial-need rule;
