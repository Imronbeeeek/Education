## How to read the deadline tags

Each checked university now shows a tag under its dates. Its "Application window" box gives the source link.

| Tag | Meaning | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| **✓ 2027 date** | Confirmed for the 2027 cycle on the university's own page or document | 123 |
| **2026 pattern** | The university's own dates for the 2026 cycle; 2027 dates are not out yet | 72 |
| **Secondary source** | From a consultancy or aggregator; confirm on the official page | 66 |
| *(no tag)* | Not checked one by one; the usual pattern for that kind of university ("typical; confirm") | 703 |

Most universities publish 2027 dates between September and December 2026. Re-check every "2026 pattern" row in late November.
