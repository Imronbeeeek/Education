# 6. Foundation & Pathway Routes (researched Oct 2026)

A foundation year is only worth taking when **the target university accepts that specific foundation**: ideally with guaranteed progression, or through a state exam recognised nationwide. Where a country already admits you directly, a foundation wastes a year.

## Where a foundation helps

| Country | Why | Best programmes |
|---|---|---|
| **Australia** | Most universities need Year 12 level; foundations give guaranteed progression | UQ College Accelerated (grade 5 in top four subjects ✔; starts 15 Feb 2027, apply by 11 Jan 2027 offshore; Sep intake by 2 Aug); Trinity College Foundation (Russian Attestat average 4 = Standard, 5 = Fast Track ✔; intakes 12 Jan, 8 Feb, July 2027); Monash College Foundation Year (IELTS 5.5, bands 5.0 ✔; 22 Feb 2027) and Diploma Part 1 (Year-11 entry → Monash Year 2); Taylors College (Sydney; Year 11 average 65%/70%; 11 Jan 2027) |
| **Italy** | The Ministry accepts a foundation that compensates the missing 12th year | University of Pavia Foundation Year (explicitly for 11 years of schooling; Oct–Jun; application windows open 1–15 Oct, so check the current cycle now; needs a Declaration of Value); Ca' Foscari; Perugia/Siena Stranieri |
| **Germany** | Only a state Feststellungsprüfung gives nationwide access | FH Aachen Freshman Program, English track (entrance exam: register by 12 Nov 2026, exam 3 Dec 2026; or TestAS by 15 Jul 2027; runs 1 Oct 2027–31 Jul 2028); CBS Prep4University Cologne (English track; visa applicants by 15 May 2027; IELTS 6.0). Constructor's private foundation leads mainly to Constructor |
| **Netherlands** | Research universities need +1 year; foundations accepted only via partners | Holland ISC: UAS Foundation Jan–Jul 2027 → Hanze (IELTS 5.5, W 5.5 ✔); B&M / S&E Foundation Sep 2027 → VU, Twente, The Hague UAS (also lists Erasmus, Tilburg, Groningen; confirm) |
| **Malaysia** | Degree entry assumes 12–13 years | MUFY at Sunway (Jan/Jul/Aug; IELTS 5.5/5.0 ✔); Taylor's Foundation |
| **UAE (UK branches)** | UK entry rules | Heriot-Watt Dubai Degree Entry (Jan/Sep; IELTS 5.5, no score lower ✔) |
| **Canada** | Pathway colleges give Year-11 entry | Fraser International College → SFU (IELTS 6.0, bands 5.5 ✔; Jan/May/Sep); ICM → U of Manitoba |
| **Malta** | Matriculation-level needed | University of Malta Foundation Studies (IELTS 5.5, R/W 5.0 ✔) |
| **Home (Tashkent)** | No visa during the bridge | NCUK International Foundation Year at STARS International University (guaranteed progression to 80+ NCUK partners incl. UNSW, UWA, RMIT, Alberta; ask about a January start); WIUT Certificate in International Foundation Studies (University of Westminster award; Sept start; deadline ~15 Jul; IELTS 6.0/W 5.0 ✔) |

## Where a foundation does NOT help

- **Already direct:** US, Sweden, Finland, Hungary, Türkiye, UAE (US-model universities), Korea, China, Hong Kong, Baltics, Portugal.
- **Norway:** requires one year of *higher education* (GSU list). A foundation does not count. **Denmark** is similar.
- **Erasmus Rotterdam & Tilburg:** state that foundation certificates are not accepted. Erasmus accepts a Kazakh Attestat + national test + maths exam, so ask whether an Uzbek Attestat + DTM is treated the same way.
- **University of Twente:** states the Uzbek Attestat is not VWO-level. Its Twente Pathway College took its **last intake in Sept 2026**.
- **VU Amsterdam VASVU:** Dutch-taught, for EU or resident students only.
- **NTU/NUS Singapore:** need AP exams or A-levels, not a foundation.
- **University of Debrecen foundation:** asks for 12 years, and Debrecen admits you directly anyway.

A February 2027 foundation in Australia finishes **no later than a direct US start**. That is why it is recommended as a parallel track, not a fallback.
