# Country-by-Country Master Institutional Mapping — Method & Legend

This folder holds one file per target country (33 total). Every file uses the same structure:

1. **Recognition verdict**: how that country's rules treat an **11-year Uzbek Attestat issued 17 June 2022** (UM № 1506508).
2. **Gap-year / recency and age rules**: whether "2022 graduate, 22 years old (23 on 8 July 2027)" triggers any exclusion.
3. **Test leverage**: where an SAT score or a higher IELTS Writing band moves an institution up a category.
4. **Category A / B / C / D mapping** of institutions, by name and by institution type.
5. **Program breakdown table** with the columns you asked for.
6. **Deadlines** for Spring 2027 (secondary choice) and Fall 2027 (main choice).
7. **Sources & confidence**.

## Category definitions (as requested)

| Code | Meaning | Practical reading for you |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Direct Entry Match**: the current profile meets direct first-year Bachelor's entry | Apply now with Attestat + current IELTS (7.5 overall; L8.0 R8.5 W5.5 S7.5). May still need routine paperwork (apostille, translation, equivalence certificate). |
| **B** | **Exam/Score Conditional Match**: eligible once a specific score is reached | Usually **IELTS Writing ≥ 6.0 or 6.5** (your only weak band), an **SAT** score, a national/university entrance test (TR-YÖS, TOPIK, HSK), or proof of the **Uzbek national university entrance test** (DTM/BMBA). |
| **C** | **Pathway / Bridge Match**: needs a foundation year, Studienkolleg, diploma, or one completed year of university | The 11-year Attestat is treated as one year short of the local university-entrance level. |
| **D** | **Ineligible**: administrative rule, age/recency limit, nationality/gender restriction, closure, or accreditation barrier | Do not spend application fees here for 2027 unless your facts differ from the assumptions below. |

Some institutions carry a compound code. Examples:

- **A\***: direct entry, but an administrative approval (homologation or equivalence) carries real refusal risk.
- **C→A**: a pathway now, but direct entry if you have already completed one year at an Uzbek university (see Scenario S2 in `strategy/02-master-matrix.md`).

## Fit Rating

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ★★★★★ | Eligible now or after one routine step. English-taught options exist. A Fall 2027 (or Spring 2027) deadline is still reachable. Strong academic match for a 5.0/5.0 applicant. |
| ★★★★ | Eligible after one achievable fix (Writing 6.0–6.5, SAT, entrance test) or one moderate administrative risk. |
| ★★★ | Feasible, but only via a one-year bridge, a new language (German/French/Italian/Korean/Chinese/Turkish), or uncertain recognition. |
| ★★ | Several stacked barriers. Treat as a long shot for 2027 and keep for 2028. |
| ★ | Effectively closed for 2027 entry. |

## How "all institutions" are covered

Literal one-by-one coverage is impossible: the US alone has about 4,000 degree-granting institutions, China about 3,000 and Türkiye 200+. Eligibility, however, is **rule-driven**. In every target country the recognition rules for a foreign secondary certificate apply by **institution type**:

- public university
- university of applied sciences / polytechnic
- private university
- foreign branch campus
- art/music academy
- military/police academy
- women-only institution

Each country file therefore:

1. states the rule that governs **every** institution of each type (so any institution you find later can be classified instantly), and
2. names as many individual institutions as is practical. Small and medium systems are listed in full or near-full. Large systems list every major public and private institution plus all the important exception cases.

## Confidence labels

- **High**: the rule was confirmed against an official university or government page, via search, during this research (Oct 2026).
- **Medium**: confirmed for a comparable 11-year CIS certificate (Russia/Kazakhstan) or by secondary sources. Uzbekistan-specific wording was not seen.
- **Low**: inferred from the system's general rule. Must be confirmed by e-mail with the admissions office before you pay a fee.

Several official sites (anabin, Nuffic, universityadmissions.se, maastrichtuniversity.nl, mofa.go.kr) could not be opened directly from the research environment. Their content was taken from search-engine extracts of those pages. Treat every rule here as a **strong starting hypothesis**, and get written confirmation from each admissions office.
