# Australia 🇦🇺

**Overall verdict:** **C** at most universities. Australian assessment treats an 11-year CIS Attestat as below Year 12, so the standard route is a **Foundation program (≈ 8–12 months)** or **one year of university/diploma**. **B** where a university directly assesses high Attestat grades or accepts the SAT. On top of that, **IELTS Writing 5.5 fails the usual "6.0 in each band" rule** for degree entry.
**Fit: ★★★½ (excellent, well-structured pathways; viable Feb/Mar 2027 start)**
**Confidence:** Medium. Russian Attestat analogues confirmed (UTS +1 year; ANU doesn't list it; Trinity College and UQ College accept it into Foundation). Uzbek-specific pages not seen.

## 1. How Australia treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Australian universities **do not all assess the qualification in the same way**:
  - some assess Grade-11 national certificates for direct entry on grades;
  - others require an extra credential (**SAT, one year of university, or Foundation**).
- **Analogues (High confidence):**
  - **UTS** requires **one completed full-time year of bachelor's study** for the Russian Attestat.
  - **ANU's** list of accepted school qualifications **does not include** the Russian certificate.
  - **Trinity College Foundation** (Melbourne pathway) accepts the Russian Attestat with **average 4** (Standard) or **5** (Fast Track). **You qualify for Fast Track.**
  - **UQ College** requires grade 4 in the top four academic subjects (✔).
- **SAT:** UNSW and ANU cite **1250** minima; Monash about 1100–1290; Sydney about 1170. These are typically paired with a **US-system diploma**. For Attestat holders, ask whether **SAT + Attestat** is accepted for direct entry. Some universities accept, most default to Foundation.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No age limits or certificate-age rules for admission.
- **Visa (subclass 500) realities:**
  - **Genuine Student (GS) requirement**: answer structured questions (about 150 words each). Officers scrutinise **"logical progression"**, so your **2022–2027 gap must be documented** (work, study, IELTS prep, courses).
  - **English test must be within 2 years of visa lodgement.** Your Sept-2025 IELTS is usable for a lodgement before **2 Sep 2027**, but the retake is advisable anyway.
  - **National Planning Level (NPL): 295,000** new international students for 2026, unchanged for 2027. Once a provider passes its allocation, visas are processed more slowly. **Apply early** for Semester 1 / Semester 2.
  - **Financial capacity:** AUD 29,710/yr (reported 2026; finance ignored per brief).
  - **Evidence level:** Uzbekistan's assessment level is set dynamically. Assume the highest documentation standard (full financials, English, GS).
- Passport must cover the CoE duration, so **renew it before lodging** (current expiry 21 Sep 2027).

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| **IELTS Writing ≥ 6.0** (most) / **6.5** (Law, Education, Health) | Converts many **B → A** for degree entry *after* Foundation. Foundation programmes need about **IELTS 5.5 overall, Writing ≥5.0** → ✔ now |
| **SAT ≥ 1250–1350** | May enable **direct entry** at UNSW, ANU, Monash, Sydney, Melbourne, Adelaide, Macquarie, if accepted with the Attestat (ask each) |
| 1 year of university (S2) | Direct entry at almost all universities, often with credit |
