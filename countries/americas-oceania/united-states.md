# United States 🇺🇸

**Overall verdict:** **A** almost everywhere. US institutions have **no 12-year rule** and routinely admit holders of completed national 11-year secondary credentials, including Uzbek and other CIS attestats. Your **IELTS 7.5 overall** meets most minimums, which are usually set on the **overall** band. Your **perfect Attestat** is a strong academic signal. The **SAT** is the single biggest upgrade: it is required again at many highly selective universities.
**Fit: ★★★★★ (largest, most flexible system for your profile)**
**Confidence:** High

## 1. How the US treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Admission is **institution-by-institution and holistic**. Completion of the home country's university-preparatory secondary education is sufficient. No federal equivalency step.
- Some universities ask for a **course-by-course credential evaluation** (WES / ECE / SpanTran), mainly public universities. Your trilingual, QR-coded Attestat simplifies this.

## 2. Gap-year / first-year vs transfer — the critical classification
| Your history since June 2022 | US classification | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **No college/university enrolment after school** (work, self-study, courses that award no degree credit) | **First-year (freshman) applicant**, regardless of gap length (Common App definition) | Apply to the full first-year pool; explain the gap in the Common App "Additional Information" section |
| **Enrolled at any university after 2022** (even one semester) | **Transfer applicant** | Different deadlines (often 1 Mar), college transcript + mid-term report; **NYU Abu Dhabi-style "no transfer" schools and some others exclude you** |

**Non-traditional route:** **Columbia University School of General Studies (GS)** admits students who have had a **break of one year or more** in their educational path. You qualify. F-1 students must study full time.
- GS deadlines (fall): **Priority Early Action 15 Jan**, **Early Action 1 Mar**, **Regular 15 May**.
- International (F-1) applicants are encouraged to use the **Early Action** deadline.

**Age:** no age limits anywhere.

## 6. Visa: F-1 status for Uzbek nationals (verified Oct 2026)
- **Proclamation 10998** (effective 1 Jan 2026) restricts entry or visas for **39 countries + Palestinian Authority documents**. **Uzbekistan is not on that list.**
  - Full restrictions: 19 countries.
  - Partial restrictions (including F/M/J): Burundi, Cuba, Togo, Venezuela, Angola, Antigua & Barbuda, Benin, Côte d'Ivoire, Dominica, Gabon, The Gambia, Malawi, Mauritania, Nigeria, Senegal, Tanzania, Tonga, Zambia, Zimbabwe.
  - Turkmenistan: immigrant-visa ban only.
- **Uzbekistan *is* on the separate January 2026 pause of *immigrant* visas (75 countries).** That pause **does not affect F-1 student visas**.
- Practical F-1 steps: I-20 → SEVIS I-901 fee → **DS-160** → interview at **US Embassy Tashkent**. Set social-media accounts to public (current State Department vetting), and show **non-immigrant intent** and finances.
- **Passport must be valid at least 6 months beyond the intended stay** (or meet the six-month-club exemption). **Renew before the interview** (current expiry 21 Sep 2027).
