# Canada 🇨🇦

**Overall verdict:**
- **A/B.** Most Canadian universities accept a completed national secondary credential that leads to university in the home country, and several explicitly list Uzbek or CIS certificates.
- The binding constraint for many top universities is the **IELTS "no band below 6.0/6.5"** rule (your Writing is 5.5). Retaking Writing turns many **B → A**.
- Separately, the **federal study-permit cap** requires a **Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL)** for undergraduates.

**Fit: ★★★★**
**Confidence:** Medium-High

## 1. How Canada treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
| University | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **University of Alberta** | Accepts for Uzbekistan the **Certificate of Completed (Upper) Secondary Education + transcript** → **A** |
| **McGill** | Its list includes the **Russian** Attestat and the **Kazakh** Attestat; **Uzbekistan is not listed**. Ask how the Uzbek certificate is assessed (likely like Russia/Kazakhstan) |
| **Western University** | Uzbekistan page lists the **Diploma of Academic Lyceum**, plus the old Certificate of Completed Secondary Education for pre-2005 graduates. This reflects the **pre-2017 12-year (9+3)** structure. A **post-2017 11-year school Attestat** may need individual review → **confirm** |
| **Toronto Metropolitan (TMU)** | Accepts the Russian Year-11 Attestat with high standing → analogous **A** |
| **UBC** | Requires graduation from an **approved university-preparatory programme**; Uzbek credentials assessed case by case |
| **University of Toronto** | Requires credentials **equivalent to the Canadian Grade-12 diploma**; country tool. Ask for the Uzbek ruling |

**Key insight:** many institutions' "Uzbekistan" guidance was written for the **old 12-year lyceum/college system**. Explicitly state in emails that your Attestat is from the **reformed 11-year general secondary school (2017+)**.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No age limits or certificate-age rules for admission.
- Study-permit officers assess the purpose of study and the gap. Document 2022–2027 activities.
- **Study-permit cap:** IRCC's 2026–2028 levels plan sets **150,000 new international-student arrivals for 2027** (155,000 in 2026). **Undergraduates need a PAL/TAL** from the province (issued via the university after acceptance). Graduate students are exempt since 1 Jan 2026.
- **Québec:** a **CAQ** (Québec Acceptance Certificate) is needed before the study permit, adding 4–8 weeks.
