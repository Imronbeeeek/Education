# Jordan 🇯🇴

**Overall verdict:** **D** for direct entry. Jordan's Tawjihi-equivalency process for foreign certificates requires **proof of 12 years of schooling**, and every Jordanian university, public and private, admits through that equivalency. **C** only if a 12th year (e.g. a completed first year of university) is accepted by the Ministry of Education (unconfirmed).
**Fit: ★**
**Confidence:** Medium (12-year proof requirement seen; Uzbek-specific ruling not seen)

## 1. How Jordan treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Foreign secondary certificates must obtain **Tawjihi equivalency** from the Ministry of Education. The Ministry converts grades to a Tawjihi-equivalent average and stream.
- **"Proof of 12 years of schooling is required"** for equivalency.
- International-student admission (studyinjordan.jo, General Policy for the Admission of International Students) also defers to the equivalency.
- Non-Tawjihi holders compete for limited quotas at public universities. International students usually enter via the **international/parallel programme** (higher fees).

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No general certificate-age limit was found (older Arab certificates are handled by specific rules).
- Programmes are mostly **Arabic-taught**, except Medicine, Engineering, IT and some Business programmes in English.

## 3. Test leverage
- SAT / IELTS: **cannot cure** the 12-year requirement. SAT is used for some international curricula in combination with a 12-year diploma.
