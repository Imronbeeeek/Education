# South Korea 🇰🇷

**Overall verdict:** **A (administratively)**. Korean Ministry of Education guidance recognises applicants who completed **their entire primary-secondary schooling in an 11-year-system country** (Uzbekistan is listed) as equivalent to Korean high-school graduates. Admission is then gated by **language** (TOPIK for Korean-taught; IELTS/TOEFL for English tracks) and, for Uzbek nationals, **visa screening**.
**Fit: ★★★★ (strong Spring-2027 and Fall-2027 option; GKS age-eligible)**
**Confidence:** Medium-High

## 1. How Korea treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Korean admissions generally state **"12 years of schooling or equivalent"**. The MOE / MOFA notice on *recognising graduates of foreign systems shorter than 12 years* classifies **Uzbekistan, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan, Mongolia, Türkiye, Malaysia, Brazil** as 11-grade systems.
  - Those who completed **the whole curriculum in such a country** are recognised as having the equivalent of Korean high-school graduation.
  - Only **mixed-system** schooling triggers an extra university-year requirement.
  - Some *university-specific* guides (e.g. for overseas-Korean special admission) still demand "high school + 1st university year" for 11-grade countries. **Confirm with each university's foreign-student guide.**
- **Foreign-national admission** (외국인 특별전형) also requires **both parents** to be foreign nationals → ✔.
- Documents: **apostilled** Attestat + supplement, translated into Korean or English. Some universities also want **consular confirmation or an online verification** (the QR code on your Attestat helps).

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No age ceiling or certificate-age rule for regular admission.
- **GKS-U (Global Korea Scholarship) 2027:**
  - **Under 25** (born after 1 Mar 2002) → ✔
  - GPA **≥80%** → ✔
  - **Embassy Track closed 30 Sep 2026**
  - **University Track runs Sep–Nov 2026** (no common deadline). **Check open university-track calls immediately**
  - Final results about **7 Jan 2027**
- **Visa (D-2) specifics for Uzbek nationals:**
  - financial proof via a **KDB Bank Uzbekistan** deposit statement (amount set by the consulate; reported ≈ USD 12,500–20,000);
  - **tuberculosis certificate**;
  - **family-relation documents** (notarised);
  - at universities under **strengthened visa screening**, Uzbek nationals must show **TOPIK 4** for bachelor's, **even for English tracks**. Certified (IEQAS) universities are exempt from this extra rule. **Prefer IEQAS-certified universities.**

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| **TOPIK** | Korean-taught programmes usually need **TOPIK 3** at entry (some 4) and **TOPIK 4** to graduate. Non-certified universities require **TOPIK 4 for Uzbek visa applicants**. TOPIK runs about 6 times a year (also in Tashkent) |
| IELTS 7.5 | English tracks typically need **IELTS 5.5–6.5** → ✔ (Writing sub-scores rarely set) |
| SAT | Optional booster for top English-track colleges (Yonsei UIC, Korea University's international division, KAIST) |
| D-4 language route | Enrol in a university **Korean Language Institute (D-4 visa)** in Dec 2026/Mar 2027 → reach TOPIK 3–4 → enter the degree in **Sept 2027 or Mar 2028** |
