# Singapore 🇸🇬

**Overall verdict:**
- **Autonomous Universities (AUs): C for 2027.** NUS and NTU require **≥12 years of general education**. **NTU explicitly states CIS high-school certificates (including Uzbekistan's) are "not suitable for direct admission"**: add **3 AP exams (scores 4/5) or 3 A-levels**, plus SAT ≥1250 / ACT ≥30 for international qualifications. Those exams sit in May/June 2027 with results in July, so this is realistically a **2028** route.
- **Private institutions (CPE-registered): A/C.** Foundation and diploma pathways to partner degrees.

**Fit: ★★ (AUs) / ★★★ (private)**
**Confidence:** High (NTU CIS rule; NUS 12-year rule)

## 1. How Singapore treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
| University | Rule |
|---|---|
| **NTU** | High-school certificates from **Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan are not suitable for direct admission**. Add **≥3 AP exams at 4–5, or 3 A-level subjects**. Minimum **SAT 1250** / **ACT 30** for international-qualification applicants. ≥12 years of general education |
| **NUS** | Must have finished high school with **at least 12 years of general education**. SAT/ACT/AP optional but considered |
| **SMU** | International qualifications plus SAT/ACT/IELTS/TOEFL/etc. Expect the same 12-year expectation |
| **SUTD, SIT, SUSS** | Similar 12-year norms |

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No statutory age limits. NUS/NTU admit the cohort finishing school in the application year or earlier. Gap years should be explained.
- ICA **Student's Pass** via the institution (SOLAR+).

## 3. Test leverage (2028 path)
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| **3 × AP (4/5)** | Turns the Attestat into an NTU-acceptable profile. AP exams are held **in May 2027** (register with an AP test centre by **about Nov 2026**). Scores arrive in July 2027, after the 2027 cycle, so they serve **2028 entry** |
| SAT ≥ 1250 (NTU minimum); 1450+ competitive | Required/recommended for international-qualification holders |
| IELTS W 5.5 | NUS/NTU typically need IELTS 6.5 overall with sub-score expectations (check). Retake Writing to 6.5 |
