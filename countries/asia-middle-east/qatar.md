# Qatar 🇶🇦

**Overall verdict:**
- **Qatar University: D.** It requires a **minimum of 12 years of formal education**.
- **Education City US branch campuses: A** (US-style admission, which accepts national 11-year diplomas).
- **Texas A&M Qatar: D** (closing; no new undergraduates).

**Fit: ★★★ (Education City)**
**Confidence:** High (QU 12-year rule; TAMUQ closure)

## 1. How Qatar treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- **Qatar University (QU):** "All applicants must complete a **minimum of 12 years of formal education**." Transcripts must be certified by the issuing authority. → **D** for an 11-year Attestat.
- **QU International Students Scholarship:** high-school average **≥95%** (you have 100%), an **embassy letter** addressed to QU, and you must meet the college requirements. Still blocked by the 12-year rule.
- **Education City** (Qatar Foundation) hosts US institutions that admit through their **home-campus standards**: Common App or own application, holistic review. US practice accepts completed national secondary education, including 11-year systems.

## 2. Gap-year / recency / age
- Education City (US practice): **first-year applicant if no college enrolment since school**. Gap years are accepted (CMU-Q has admitted gap-year students); explain the 2022–2027 period.
- If you enrolled at any university after 2022 → you apply as a **transfer** (≥1 year of full-time college work, B average).

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
