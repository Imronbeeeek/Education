# China 🇨🇳

**Overall verdict:** **A**. Chinese universities admit international undergraduates with a **high-school graduation certificate** of any national system, subject to age windows (commonly 18–25 or 18–30) and language (HSK for Chinese-taught, IELTS ~6.0 for English-taught). Elite universities add written exams or interviews.
**Fit: ★★★★**
**Confidence:** High (Tsinghua, PKU and CSC rules seen)

## 1. How China treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- The Ministry of Education's international-student framework requires: **non-Chinese citizenship, a valid passport, a senior high-school graduation certificate, good health**. **No 12-year rule.** Uzbek attestats are routinely accepted; Central Asian students are a major cohort.
- Elite universities run their own **assessment** (written tests in Maths/Physics/Chemistry or Chinese/English, plus interviews, often online). Tsinghua and PKU publish "with" or "without written examination" tracks.

| Lever | Effect |

### Category B: exam/language conditional
- **Chinese-taught programmes:** HSK 4/5, or a one-year **preparatory year**.
- **Tsinghua / PKU / Fudan / SJTU / ZJU:** written exams plus interview; very competitive.

### Category C
- **CSC preparatory year** (Chinese language + subject foundation) for Chinese-taught degrees: built into the scholarship.

### Category D
- **Military/defence universities** (e.g. National University of Defense Technology), **public-security universities**: closed to foreign civilians.
- Programmes whose stated upper age limit is below 23 (rare; check).
