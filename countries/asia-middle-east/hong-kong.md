# Hong Kong 🇭🇰

**Overall verdict:**
- **A** at **HKBU**, which explicitly lists the Uzbek Attestat.
- **A/B** at **HKU**: individual merit assessment; your IELTS already meets HKU's "6.0, no subtest below 5.5".
- **B** at **CUHK / PolyU / HKUST / CityU**. For comparable CIS certificates they expect high averages, often plus national exam results. For you that means the **Uzbek national test**, SAT, or similar evidence.

**Fit: ★★★★**
**Confidence:** High (HKBU, HKU English rule, deadlines); Medium (others)

## 1. How Hong Kong treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- International applicants apply **directly to each university** (non-JUPAS). Hong Kong has no 12-year statutory rule; each university publishes a country list.

| University | Rule seen for Uzbekistan or comparable certificates |
|---|---|
| **HKBU** | Uzbekistan: *Umumiy o'rta ta'lim to'g'risida attestat / shahodatnoma / Аттестат об общем среднем образовании* **or** Akademik litsey diplomi → **accepted** (plus English requirement) |
| **CUHK** | Russia: Attestat with **average 4.5/5**, plus high EGE marks recommended. Kazakhstan: grade 4 in each subject **plus UNT ≥ 80%**. By analogy for Uzbekistan: your 5.0/5.0 is excellent, but a **national test result** strengthens the file |
| **PolyU** | Russia/Kazakhstan: Attestat **and** national test (EGE/UNT) |
| **HKU** | "Other national qualifications" assessed **on individual merit** |
| **HKU (mature route)** | Applicants **aged 25+** on 1 Sep of entry are deemed to satisfy entrance requirements. **Not applicable** (you will be 23) |

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age limits. Explain 2022–2027 in the personal statement (work, self-study, IELTS, etc.).
- **English test validity:** CUHK requires results **valid on the application submission date** (two years from the test date). Your 2 Sep 2025 IELTS is valid for 2026–27 submissions.

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 (W 5.5) | **HKU: overall 6.0, no subtest < 5.5 → ✔**. HKBU/others typically 6.0–6.5 with sub-scores ≥5.5 (check). Some programmes (Law, Medicine, English) require 7.0 with 6.5+ Writing |
| SAT | Strong supporting evidence for HKUST/CUHK/HKU, especially for an unfamiliar curriculum. Target **1400+** for HKUST/HKU engineering/business |
| Uzbek national test | Analogue of EGE/UNT for CUHK/PolyU |

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Category A: direct entry
- **Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU)**: Uzbek Attestat explicitly accepted.
- **The University of Hong Kong (HKU)**: individual assessment; English ✔.
- **Lingnan University**, **Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK)**: international qualifications reviewed individually (likely A).
- **Self-financing universities:** Hong Kong Metropolitan University (HKMU), Hang Seng University of Hong Kong (HSUHK), Hong Kong Shue Yan University, Saint Francis University (formerly Caritas Institute of HE), Tung Wah College, Chu Hai College of Higher Education, Gratia Christian College, THEi (Technological & Higher Education Institute of HK, VTC).

### Category B: score/document conditional
- **CUHK**, **PolyU**, **HKUST**, **CityU**: strong Attestat **plus** SAT / national test likely needed to be competitive. Some programmes need Writing ≥6.0–6.5.
- **HKAPA (Academy for Performing Arts):** auditions.

### Category C: pathway
- **Associate degree / Higher Diploma** at HKU SPACE Community College, CUHK School of Continuing & Professional Studies (CUSCS), PolyU SPEED/HKCC, UOW College Hong Kong, then **senior-year (Year 3) entry** into UGC universities.

### Category D
- **Centennial College HK** (closed). Programmes reserved for local (HKDSE/JUPAS) candidates.
