# United Arab Emirates 🇦🇪

**Overall verdict:** **A**. Every UAE HEI requires a **Ministry equivalency certificate** for foreign secondary diplomas, and the Ministry's rules for **CIS countries (Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan)** accept a recognised certificate from the **11-year programme**. The minimum average is "7" (70%) in the final year or the best two of the last three years.
**Exceptions:**
- **NYU Abu Dhabi:** *de facto* **D**. Applicants who graduated **two or more years prior are discouraged**.
- **Federal institutions** (HCT etc.) are for Emiratis.
- **UK/Australian branch campuses** may still require their own foundation year.

**Fit: ★★★★½**
**Confidence:** High (equivalency for CIS 11-year certificates; AUS/Khalifa/NYUAD rules)

## 1. How the UAE treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- **Certificate of Equivalency** from the UAE Ministry (MoE / MoHESR, online). All universities and higher-education institutions, public and private, mandate a Grade-12 equivalency certificate for undergraduate admission. For the CIS block, the 11-year certificate **is** the recognised Grade-12 equivalent.
- **Documents:**
  - attested graduation certificate + transcripts for the **last 3 years**, **or** Grade-9 certificate + the last 2 years;
  - **legalisation:** the UAE is **not** an Apostille party → **consular attestation chain**: Uzbek MFA → UAE Embassy in Tashkent → UAE MoFA on arrival.
- **Grade conversion:** 5.0/5.0 → 100%. Clears AUS (≥80%) and every other threshold.
