# Saudi Arabia 🇸🇦

**Overall verdict:**
- **Government universities, which admit internationals mainly via scholarships: D (most), borderline at "≤5-year" institutions.** Your age (22) is inside the **17–25** window, but the **secondary-certificate recency** rule (**≤5 years**, sometimes **≤3 years**) and Arabic-medium instruction are the binding constraints.
- **Private universities (self-funded): A/C (verify).**

**Fit: ★½**
**Confidence:** High (IUM: age 17–25 and ≤5-year certificate); Medium (system-wide)

## 1. How Saudi Arabia treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- A high-school diploma "or its equivalent" is required. The Saudi Ministry of Education equates foreign certificates. Most programmes start with an **Arabic preparatory year** at government universities.
- International places are mostly **fully-funded scholarships**, applied for via the **"Study in Saudi"** platform and university portals.
- Saudi Arabia acceded to the Apostille Convention (2022), so an **apostille** suffices.

## 2. Gap-year / recency / age (the binding rules)
| Rule | Value | You |
|---|---|---|
| Age (bachelor's scholarships, e.g. Islamic University of Madinah, KSU) | **17–25** | ✔ (22/23) |
| Certificate recency (IUM; widely used) | **No more than 5 years since obtaining the diploma** | Attestat dated **17 Jun 2022** → 5 years = **17 Jun 2027**. Eligible only if the application/decision falls **before mid-June 2027**; ineligible for later cycles |
| Certificate recency (some universities/programmes) | **≤ 3 years** | ✖ |
| Grade | "Very good" or higher | ✔ (100%) |

## 3. Test leverage
- **None** cures the recency rule. Arabic proficiency (or a prep year) is needed for Arabic-taught programmes.
- English-taught: KFUPM (STEM), Alfaisal, PSU and PMU use English (IELTS ~5.5–6.5 → ✔).

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Borderline / time-critical (apply in the early-2027 cycle only)
- **Islamic University of Madinah (IUM)**: age 17–25 ✔; certificate ≤5 years (must apply before mid-June 2027); Arabic/Islamic sciences, plus engineering/computer science (Arabic preparatory year).
- **King Saud University**, **King Abdulaziz University**, **Umm Al-Qura University**, **Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University**, **Qassim University**, **Taibah University**: international scholarship programmes. Check each university's recency rule (some use 3 years).
- **King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM)**: English-medium STEM; international scholarships are very competitive. Check age/recency.

### Category A/C: private universities (self-funded; verify recency and equivalence)
- **Alfaisal University** (Riyadh; English)
- **Prince Sultan University** (Riyadh; English)
- **Prince Mohammad bin Fahd University** (Al-Khobar; English)
- Al Yamamah University; Dar Al Uloom University; University of Business & Technology (Jeddah); Riyadh Elm University; Al Maarefa University; Arab Open University – Saudi Arabia; Sulaiman Al Rajhi University; Batterjee Medical College; Ibn Sina National College; Fakeeh College for Medical Sciences

### Category D
- **Women-only universities:** Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University, Effat University, Dar Al-Hekma University.
- **KAUST**: graduate only.
- **King Saud bin Abdulaziz University for Health Sciences**: Saudi-focused.
- **Military/security colleges.**
- **Any programme with a ≤3-year recency rule.**
- **All government-university scholarship cycles after mid-June 2027.**

**Other government universities** (all subject to the same recency/age rules): King Khalid, Jazan, Najran, Al-Jouf, Northern Border, Tabuk, Ha'il, Al-Baha, Shaqra, Majmaah, Prince Sattam bin Abdulaziz, Imam Abdulrahman bin Faisal, King Faisal, University of Jeddah, Taif, Bisha, Hafr Al-Batin, Saudi Electronic University.
