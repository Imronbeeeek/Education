# Malaysia 🇲🇾

**Overall verdict:** **C** for most degree programmes. Malaysian degree entry assumes **12–13 years** (STPM/A-level/Foundation/Diploma), and an 11-year Attestat is treated like the Malaysian SPM (Form 5) level, so a **Foundation year (≈12 months) or a Diploma** is needed.
**Exception, A:** **Universiti Utara Malaysia (UUM)** accepts government-school certificates covering **11–12 years**, but such certificates are **valid for only 5 years from the exam year** (yours: 2022 → valid through 2027).
**Fit: ★★★ (fast, flexible pathways; strong Spring 2027 option)**
**Confidence:** High (UM 12-year rule; UUM 11–12-year + 5-year validity rule)

## 1. How Malaysia treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
| Institution | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Universiti Malaya (UM)** | Minimum: **completed 12 years** + STPM/A-Level/IB/High School Grade 12/equivalent, **or** a recognised Diploma with CGPA ≥3.00 → **C** |
| **Universiti Utara Malaysia (UUM)** | "Senior High School / Senior Secondary School / other certificates from government schools **with at least 11 to 12 years of study** … **valid for 5 years from the year of Higher Secondary Examination**" → **A until the 5-year window closes** |
| **Private universities / branch campuses** | Foundation programmes accept SPM/O-level-equivalents. The Attestat is normally accepted into **Foundation** (e.g. Monash University Foundation Year at Sunway College) |
| **UK branch campuses** (Nottingham, Southampton, Heriot-Watt, Reading*) | UK rules: 11-year systems → **Foundation** |
| **Australian branch campuses** (Monash, Curtin, Swinburne) | Australian rules: Foundation or +1 year |

\*Verify the current intake status of the University of Reading Malaysia.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- **UUM 5-year validity:** 2022 exam + 5 → **2027**. A **February/March 2027** intake is safely inside the window. **September/October 2027** is borderline: confirm whether "valid for 5 years" means through the end of 2027.
- **EMGS student pass:** no statutory maximum age (undergraduates are typically 18–35).
- Medical exams and EMGS approval take about 4–8 weeks.

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 | Far above Malaysian needs (UM: **IELTS 5.0**; private universities 5.5–6.0) → ✔ |
| SAT / AP | Some private universities and branch campuses may grant direct entry to an "American High School + SAT/AP" profile. **For an Attestat holder, SAT alone rarely replaces Foundation.** Ask Taylor's, Sunway and Monash Malaysia |
| Foundation result | **The real lever**: guaranteed progression at the same university |
