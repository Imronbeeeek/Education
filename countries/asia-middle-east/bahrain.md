# Bahrain 🇧🇭

**Overall verdict:**
- **University of Bahrain: D.** High-school graduation must be **no more than two years before the application year**, and yours is from 2022.
- **Private universities: A/C (uncertain).** They require a secondary-certificate equivalence via the Ministry of Education / Higher Education Council. Whether an 11-year certificate is accepted as Grade-12-equivalent has **not been confirmed**.

**Fit: ★½**
**Confidence:** High (UoB recency rule); Low (private-sector equivalence)

## 1. How Bahrain treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Admission to any Bahraini HEI requires the secondary certificate to be **authenticated** in the issuing country and **evaluated / equated** under Bahraini rules (Ministry of Education equivalence; HEC oversight for private HEIs).
- Gulf equivalence practice is built around **Grade 12**. Some GCC regulators accept CIS 11-year certificates as complete secondary education (the UAE does; see the UAE file); others do not. **Bahrain: not verified. Ask the HEC / target university.**
- Uzbekistan and Bahrain are both Hague Apostille parties: **apostille** the Attestat.
