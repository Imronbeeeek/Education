# Türkiye 🇹🇷

**Overall verdict:** **A**. Under YÖK's 2026 rules for admitting students from abroad, universities may admit **on the secondary-diploma grade** (≥50/100 for most programmes; **≥85/100 for Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy**) **and/or TR-YÖS, SAT, ACT** and other recognised scores. Your **100%** Attestat clears every diploma-grade threshold. The MEB *denklik* (equivalence) for 11-year systems is a routine step.
**Exception: Türkiye Bursları scholarship is D** (undergraduate age limit under 21).
**Fit: ★★★★½**
**Confidence:** High

## 1. How Türkiye treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- **YÖK "2026 Esasları"** (principles for admitting students from abroad):
  - Universities publish international quotas and choose their evaluation instruments: **TR-YÖS** (central exam by ÖSYM), **diploma grade**, **SAT/ACT**, **IB/Abitur/A-level**, and others.
  - **Public universities no longer run their own YÖS exams.** They use TR-YÖS or accepted international scores/diploma averages.
  - **Diploma-grade route minima:** **85/100** for Medicine, Dentistry and Pharmacy; **50/100** for other bachelor's and associate programmes.
- **Lise Denklik Belgesi** (MEB equivalence of the secondary diploma) is mandatory for registration. MEB processes equivalence for countries with 11-year primary+secondary systems. Apply via **e-Denklik** (edenklik.meb.gov.tr) or Turkish education attachés (Tashkent).
- Uzbekistan and Türkiye are both Apostille parties → **apostille + notarised Turkish translation**.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No age ceiling and no certificate-age rule for regular admission.
- **Türkiye Bursları (YTB) undergraduate: applicants must be under 21 → D.**
- Student residence permit after enrolment (Göç İdaresi).

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Diploma grade 100%** | Meets all YÖK minima. Many foundation (vakıf) and public universities rank international applicants on it |
| **SAT** | Top publics prefer **1350+** (METU/Boğaziçi). **Koç** general minimum about **1200**, CS higher (about 1260–1450). Sabancı and Bilkent accept SAT in lieu of YÖS |
| **TR-YÖS 2027** | Session 1: **apply 1–15 Mar 2027** (late until 23 Mar), **exam 16 May 2027**, results **10 Jun 2027**. Session 2: apply 15–28 Sep 2027, exam 28 Nov 2027 (for 2028) |
| English proficiency | English-medium programmes require IELTS **6.0–6.5** (or internal exam), otherwise a **one-year English prep**. **METU/Boğaziçi often require 6.5 with sub-score floors**, so a Writing retake helps |
| Turkish | Turkish-medium programmes need **TÖMER C1** or a one-year Turkish prep. As an Uzbek (Turkic-language) speaker, you will learn fast |

### Category B: score conditional
- **METU, Boğaziçi, ITU, Koç, Sabancı, Bilkent** for competitive majors (CS, EE, Industrial Engineering, Economics): **SAT 1350–1450+**; English proficiency ≥ IELTS 6.5 to skip prep.
- **Medicine / Dentistry / Pharmacy:** diploma ≥85% ✔, but extremely competitive. TR-YÖS/SAT used for ranking.

### Category C
- **English/Turkish preparatory year** (hazırlık) at most universities if proficiency isn't proven. It is built in, not a barrier.

### Category D
- **Türkiye Bursları** (age < 21).
- **National Defence University**, **Police Academy**, **Gendarmerie and Coast Guard Academy**: Turkish citizenship.
