# Kuwait 🇰🇼

**Overall verdict:**
- **Kuwait University: D.** The international-scholarship route requires a high-school certificate **issued within the last 2 years**; other non-Kuwaiti places are extremely limited.
- **Private universities: A/C (uncertain).** They need a **Kuwait Ministry of Education equivalency** of the foreign certificate (11-year acceptance unconfirmed) and **university-sponsored student residency**.

**Fit: ★½**
**Confidence:** High (KU scholarship recency); Low (private-sector equivalency)

## 1. How Kuwait treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Foreign certificates must be **evaluated by Kuwait's Ministry of Education as equivalent** to the Kuwaiti general secondary certificate (science or arts stream). Non-academic subjects are excluded from the percentage calculation; your academic core is 5/5 anyway.
- Whether the Ministry equates an **11-year** certificate is **not confirmed**. Gulf practice varies (the UAE accepts the CIS 11-year attestat; Qatar University does not).
- Uzbekistan is a Hague Apostille party; Kuwait is **not**. Expect **consular legalisation** (Uzbek MFA → Kuwaiti embassy) rather than an apostille.
