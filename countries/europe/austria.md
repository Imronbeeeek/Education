# Austria 🇦🇹

**Overall verdict:** C by default. It moves to **A/B** if you can prove you passed (or were admitted through) Uzbekistan's national university-entrance test for the field you are applying to.
**Fit: ★★★ (★★★★ for English-taught programmes if the entrance-test proof exists)**
**Confidence:** Medium

## 1. How Austria treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat

Austrian law (Universitätsgesetz §64–65) requires two separate things from third-country applicants.

| Requirement | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **General university entrance qualification** (*allgemeine Universitätsreife*) | The Attestat must be equivalent to the Austrian *Reifeprüfung*. Austria does not apply a mechanical "12-year" count. The rectorate decides equivalence and **may impose supplementary exams** (*Ergänzungsprüfungen*), typically in Mathematics, a foreign language or German, where the curriculum falls short. |
| **Special university entrance qualification** (*besondere Universitätsreife / Studienplatznachweis*) | Proof that you **would be admitted to the same programme in Uzbekistan**, including any entrance exam there. Austrian guidance states that subject-specific requirements such as entrance exams "must be fulfilled in the state in which the certificate was issued" (see oesterreich.gv.at / BMFWF). For Uzbekistan this means a **result sheet or admission letter from the national entrance test** run by the Agency for Assessment of Knowledge and Skills (formerly DTM / State Testing Centre), in the **same or a related field**. |

**Consequences:**

- **You took the national entrance test in 2022 (or later) and scored an admission result (state grant or contract place):** both conditions are normally met, apart from German for German-taught programmes. → **A** (English programmes) / **B** (German programmes, German C1 needed).
- **You never took or passed it:** the university can admit you only after **supplementary exams**, prepared at a *Vorstudienlehrgang*:
  - **VWU** (*Vorstudienlehrgang der Wiener Universitäten*) for Vienna,
  - equivalent courses in Graz, Innsbruck and Leoben.

  These courses are **taught in German**. → **C**.
- **Universities of Applied Sciences (FH) and private universities** use their own equivalence checks and admission tests. In practice they are more flexible about the "special qualification" proof, but they still expect a certificate that opens university access at home.

## 2. Gap-year / age

- **No statutory time limit** on how old the secondary certificate is. The 2022 issue date is not a problem.
- **No age ceiling** for degree programmes. Exceptions: some performing-arts programmes at **mdw**, **Mozarteum** and **Kunstuni Graz** publish maximum entry ages for certain instrumental and vocal tracks. These are irrelevant unless you apply in music.
- The residence permit (*Aufenthaltsbewilligung Student*) is applied for from abroad and takes about 2–3 months. Your passport must cover the permit period, so **renew it first**.

## 3. Test leverage

| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 overall (current) | Already meets WU's BBE requirement (IELTS 6+) and typical English-programme B2/C1 rules. Austrian programmes rarely set a Writing sub-score. |
| SAT | **No formal effect** on the legal equivalence. It can strengthen selective admissions (WU BBE, CEU, Modul). |
| Uzbek national test result | **The decisive lever.** Retrieve your 2022 result through the Agency's portal if you sat it. If you did not, the 2027 national test (late July–August) is too late for Winter Semester 2027, but valid for 2028. |
| German B2/C1 | Unlocks the ~95% of bachelor's programmes that are German-taught. |

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Category A: Direct entry (conditional on the national-test proof for public universities)
- **WU Vienna – BSc Business and Economics (BBE, English).** Selection procedure held once a year in spring. Registration for the 2026/27 round opened 2 Mar 2026, so expect **about early Mar 2027**. 240 places. IELTS 6.0+. Also needs the national-test proof.
- **JKU Linz – BSc Artificial Intelligence** (English-taught).
- **University of Klagenfurt – BSc Robotics & AI** (English-taught; check current language status).
- **Private universities with English bachelor's** (own admission, generally flexible on the "special qualification"):
  - Webster Vienna Private University (US-accredited BA/BS)
  - Modul University Vienna (BBA, BSc)
  - **CEU – Central European University PU** (Vienna; BA Philosophy, Politics & Economics; BA Culture, Politics & Society; BSc Data Science & Society)
  - Lauder Business School
  - Sigmund Freud PU (some English tracks)

### Category B: Score/exam conditional
- **Any German-taught public university bachelor's** once German C1 is proven. The national-test proof is still required.
- **Medicine/Dentistry** at MedUni Vienna, Graz and Innsbruck: **MedAT** entrance test. Only about 5% of places go to third-country nationals.
- **FH programmes in English** with their own admission tests, e.g.:
  - MCI Innsbruck (Business & Management, English)
  - IMC Krems (English bachelor's in business and health management)
  - FH Joanneum, FH Campus Wien, FH Technikum Wien, FH Kufstein, FH Upper Austria (Hagenberg), FH Salzburg, FH Kärnten (CUAS), FH Vorarlberg, FHWien der WKW, FH St. Pölten, FH Burgenland, FH Wiener Neustadt (selected English tracks)

### Category C: Pathway required (no national-test proof)
All **22 public universities**, via supplementary exams after the VWU or an equivalent Vorstudienlehrgang (German-taught):

| Group | Universities |
|---|---|
| Comprehensive | University of Vienna, University of Graz, University of Innsbruck, University of Salzburg, JKU Linz, University of Klagenfurt |
| Technical / specialised | TU Wien, TU Graz, Montanuniversität Leoben, BOKU, University of Veterinary Medicine Vienna, WU Vienna |
| Medical | MedUni Vienna, Graz, Innsbruck |
| Continuing education | University for Continuing Education Krems |
| Arts | University of Applied Arts Vienna, mdw, Mozarteum Salzburg, Kunstuni Graz, Kunstuni Linz, Academy of Fine Arts Vienna |

### Category D: Ineligible
- **Theresian Military Academy** and police/public-administration FH tracks: Austrian citizenship required.
- **Performing-arts programmes with published age caps**: check only if relevant.
- Programmes with a German-language entrance exam you cannot pass by 2027.

## 7. Action items specific to Austria

1. Locate your **2022 national entrance-test result** (if any) and get an official printout or certificate. Austria, Belgium (KU Leuven), Lithuania (VILNIUS TECH) and Hong Kong (CUHK/PolyU) all value it.
2. Ask WU, JKU and CEU by e-mail: *"Is an Uzbek Attestat (11 years, 2022) without/with a national entrance-test result accepted for the BBE / AI bachelor's? Which supplementary exams would apply?"*
3. Legalisation: Uzbekistan is a Hague Apostille state, so the Attestat and supplement need an **apostille**. A **sworn translation is required only if the document is not in German or English**. Your Attestat already carries official English text, so ask whether that suffices.
