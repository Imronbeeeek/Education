# Denmark 🇩🇰

**Overall verdict:** **C** for university (universitet) bachelor's. The Attestat almost certainly has to be supplemented with **one year of higher education**, or specific Danish A/B-level subject courses. **A/B possible** at some business academies (erhvervsakademier) and university colleges (professionshøjskoler); verify each.
**Fit: ★★ (★★★★ if you have already completed one year at an Uzbek university)**
**Confidence:** Medium-Low (Uzbek-specific Danish assessment not seen; rule inferred from the official Danish framework)

## 1. How Denmark treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- A foreign qualification must be **at the level of a Danish upper-secondary leaving exam** (gymnasium/STX, 12 years in total).
- The Danish Agency for Higher Education's framework says that for some non-EU countries the school certificate **"is not enough and needs to be supplemented with one or two years of higher education, or equivalent supplementary studies."**
- The University of Copenhagen (KU) adds that you may supplement with "one or two years of higher education **or** at least one A-level and one B-level subject course."
- University guidance (e.g. SDU) notes that **a year of higher education is often required for 3-year bachelor's when the exam is from outside the EU/EEA.**
- The Danish *Eksamenshåndbogen* (exam handbook) records country-specific assessments. An 11-year CIS certificate is typically placed **below gymnasium level**.

**Result:** treat Danish universities as **Category C**. If you already have **≥1 year of successful study at an accredited Uzbek university**, many programmes become **A** (Scenario S2).

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age or age limit.
- A gap with documented work experience can help in **Quota 2** (holistic assessment).

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 | Danish "English B" equals about IELTS 6.5 overall; "English A" (some programmes) about 7.0+. **No Writing sub-score rule** at most institutions, so ✔ |
| SAT | Not part of Danish admission rules. Can support a Quota 2 file only marginally |
| Danish | Needed only for Danish-taught programmes ("Danish A" / Studieprøven) |
