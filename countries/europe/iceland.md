# Iceland 🇮🇸

**Overall verdict:** **C (likely)**. Icelandic universities require the equivalent of the Icelandic **stúdentspróf** (matriculation exam, reached after about 13 years of schooling). An 11-year Attestat is expected to fall short, and almost all undergraduate teaching is in Icelandic.
**Fit: ★½**
**Confidence:** Low-Medium (individual ENIC/NARIC Iceland assessment; no Uzbek-specific ruling seen)

## 1. How Iceland treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- General requirement: **completion of the equivalent of the Icelandic stúdentspróf** (10 compulsory + 3 upper-secondary years).
- Foreign certificates are evaluated individually. An 11-year certificate is about 2 years shorter, so expect a demand for **1–2 years of university study** or a recognised pre-university qualification (IB/A-levels).
- **Non-EU/EEA students are admitted in the autumn semester only** (Reykjavík University).

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age or age rule.
- **Non-EEA deadline: 1 Feb** (University of Iceland). Certified transcripts must arrive by that date.
- Residence permit from the Directorate of Immigration after admission.
