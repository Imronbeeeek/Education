# Latvia 🇱🇻

> **Update 8 Oct 2026:** deadlines depend on citizenship (University of Latvia 2026: 1 May or 21 Jun; RTU: 1 May, 15 Jun or 15 Jul). Plan for 1 May 2027. See [`strategy/13-tier3-oct-2026.md`](../../strategy/13-tier3-oct-2026.md).

**Overall verdict:** **A**. Latvian institutions admit holders of a secondary certificate that gives university access in the home country, after **AIC recognition** (about 1 month). English-taught bachelor's are plentiful, and the IELTS bar (5.5–6.0) is already met.
**Fit: ★★★★**
**Confidence:** High (University of Latvia names the Uzbek attestat for bachelor's)

## 1. How Latvia treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- The University of Latvia's country-specific requirements: applicants from Uzbekistan present the **secondary school attestat** as officially certified copies in the original language and in English.
- Foreign diplomas are recognised by the **Academic Information Centre (AIC)**, Latvia's ENIC/NARIC. Processing is about one month, longer for some countries.
- No 12-year rule is applied to secondary certificates that give full university access at home.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age rule and no age ceiling.
- Residence-permit documents can be filed at the **Embassy of Latvia in Uzbekistan** (Tashkent) or via the university, which is convenient.
- **Due-diligence note:** Latvia has tightened oversight of international recruitment at some private colleges. Check that the institution and programme are **accredited** in the AIKA / AIC registers before paying.

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 (W 5.5) | RTU: IELTS **5.5–6.0** / TOEFL 70–80. LU: B2. ✔ |
| Maths test | **RTU requires an online maths entrance test for all bachelor's** (plus at least one reference) |
| SAT | Not required |
| Russian fluency | Helpful in daily life. Since recent language-law changes, state and private HEIs teach in Latvian/English (EU languages), **not Russian** |

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Category A: Direct entry
**State universities:**
- **University of Latvia (LU)**: English bachelor's in Business, Computer Science, Economics, etc.
- **Riga Technical University (RTU)** (incl. RTU Riga Business School, RTU Rēzekne Academy): English bachelor's in IT, Engineering, Aviation, Business
- **Rīga Stradiņš University (RSU)**: Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy (entrance exams), Public Health, International Relations / Communication (English)
- **Latvia University of Life Sciences and Technologies (LBTU)**
- **Daugavpils University**, **Liepāja University**
- **Ventspils University of Applied Sciences** (IT, translation)
- **Vidzeme University of Applied Sciences**
- Art/music academies: Art Academy of Latvia, Jāzeps Vītols Latvian Academy of Music, Latvian Academy of Culture (audition/portfolio → B)

**Private / independent:**
- **Stockholm School of Economics in Riga (SSE Riga)**: BSc Economics & Business. Highly selective, own **entrance exams** → effectively **B**
- **BA School of Business and Finance**: verify its current organisational status (reported integration with LU)
- **RISEBA University**, **Turība University**, **Transport and Telecommunication Institute (TSI)**, **EKA University of Applied Sciences**, **ISMA University of Applied Sciences**, **Riga Graduate School of Law** (mainly graduate)

### Category B: Exam conditional
- RSU Medicine/Dentistry (entrance exams); SSE Riga (entrance exams); arts academies (auditions).

### Category C
- Not needed.

### Category D
- **National Defence Academy of Latvia**: Latvian nationality.
- Any institution whose accreditation has lapsed: check the register.
