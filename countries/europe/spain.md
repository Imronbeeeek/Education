# Spain 🇪🇸

**Overall verdict:**
- **Public universities: A\* (direct entry, subject to homologation).** Non-EU school certificates must be **homologated** to the Spanish *Título de Bachiller* by the Ministry of Education. Spain checks equivalence of **duration and content**. CIS 11-year attestats (Russia's "11th-grade Attestat") are generally treated as Bachiller-equivalent, but a refusal or conditional decision is a real risk for the 11-year Uzbek certificate.
- **Private universities: A.** They can enrol you while homologation is pending.

**Fit: ★★★½**
**Confidence:** Medium

## 1. How Spain treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Non-EU students "without international agreements" must request **homologation** of their secondary studies to the **Spanish Bachiller**. They file with the Ministry of Education, Vocational Training & Sports, a Spanish embassy/consulate, or delegated regional offices.
- **Requirements** (UNED summary):
  - the **same years of schooling** as the Spanish Bachiller (12 nominal years);
  - legalised academic certificates for the **last two years**, translated;
  - proof of passing any **final examination** of the foreign system.
- **Evidence for CIS 11-year systems:** the Spanish Ministry's office for Russia lists the Russian *Attestat o srednem obshchem obrazovanii* (11 years) as the comparator for the Spanish Bachiller. In practice, CIS attestats are homologated.
- **Risk for you:** an Uzbek post-2017 11-year certificate is not explicitly tabulated in what was seen. Treat it as **likely approvable but not guaranteed**. Apply early.
- **After homologation:** obtain the **UNEDasiss accreditation** (computes your admission grade). Optionally sit the **PCE** (*Pruebas de Competencias Específicas*) to raise your grade up to 14 for competitive programmes.
- **Provisional enrolment:** a *volante de inscripción condicional* lets you enrol while homologation is in process (public universities, subject to the regional rules).
- **Timing:** the legal maximum for resolution is 6 months under RD 889/2022 for university titles; school homologations can also take months.
- **Order of steps:** apostille **first**, then sworn translation (*traducción jurada*) into Spanish.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age rule and no age ceiling.
- Visa: Spanish student visa via BLS/consulate. Your passport must cover the stay, so **renew it before applying**.

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| PCE (UNEDasiss) | Raises the admission grade for public universities (useful for competitive degrees) |
| IELTS 7.5 | English-taught tracks require B2–C1 → ✔; Writing rarely sub-scored |
| SAT | Accepted as supporting evidence at **IE / ESADE / private schools**. IE also has its own **IE Global Admissions Test** |
| Spanish | Needed for Spanish-taught programmes (B2) |

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Category A: private universities (enrol pending homologation)
- **IE University** (Madrid/Segovia): English bachelor's in Business, IR, Economics, CS & AI, Data & Business Analytics, Architecture, Law, etc. Homologation documents due by IE's deadline (about 31 Oct of the first year); **you can enrol before homologation is complete**.
- **ESADE** (Barcelona/Madrid): BBA, Global Governance, etc. (requires homologation)
- **Universidad de Navarra** (some English tracks)
- **Universidad Europea** (Madrid/Valencia/Canarias): many English programmes
- **Universidad Nebrija**; **Universidad Francisco de Vitoria**; **Universidad CEU San Pablo**; **Universidad Camilo José Cela**; **Universidad Pontificia Comillas (ICADE – E-2/E-4 bilingual)**; **UNIE**; **ESIC**
- **US-accredited:** **Saint Louis University – Madrid** (US degree, US-style admission)
- **Swiss/other-accredited private schools:** EU Business School (Barcelona), Les Roches Marbella (hospitality), GBSB Global. **Check that the degree is officially recognised** if you need a Spanish/EU-recognised title.

### Category A\*: public universities (homologation + UNEDasiss; Spanish or English tracks)
- **Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M)**: English/bilingual groups in Economics, Business, Engineering, Law
- **Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF)**: Global Studies, Business Management/Economics (English groups)
- **Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (UAM)**; **Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB)**: some English programmes
- **Universidad Complutense de Madrid**; **Universitat de Barcelona**; **Universitat de València**; **Universidad de Granada**; **Universidad de Sevilla**; **Universidad Politécnica de Madrid**; **Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya**; **Universitat Politècnica de València**
- All other public universities (≈50): Salamanca, Zaragoza, País Vasco, Santiago de Compostela, Oviedo, Murcia, Alicante, Málaga, Cádiz, Córdoba, Valladolid, Castilla-La Mancha, Extremadura, Las Palmas, La Laguna, Rey Juan Carlos, Alcalá, Jaume I, Rovira i Virgili, Lleida, Girona, Illes Balears, etc.
- Mostly **Spanish-taught** → add Spanish B2.

### Category C
- If homologation is refused: complete **one year of university** (Uzbekistan or a private Spanish university) and request partial recognition (*convalidación*) of credits, or use private-university admission.

### Category D
- **Military academies** (AGM Zaragoza, ENM Marín, AGA San Javier): Spanish nationality.
