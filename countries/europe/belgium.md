# Belgium 🇧🇪

**Overall verdict:**
- **Flemish Community:** A/B. Direct entry is possible when the Attestat is backed by proof of university access in Uzbekistan (national-test result).
- **French Community (Wallonia-Brussels):** A only after a formal equivalence decision, and teaching is in French.

**Fit: ★★★ (Flanders English programmes ★★★★ with national-test proof)**
**Confidence:** Medium

## 1. How Belgium treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat

Belgium has **two separate higher-education systems**.

### Flemish Community (KU Leuven, UGent, VUB, UAntwerp, UHasselt + hogescholen)
- **No central equivalence step** (NARIC-Vlaanderen does level recognition, but universities admit autonomously).
- KU Leuven's rule of thumb: the **secondary diploma must grant access to similar university studies in your home country**. Its country guidance points Uzbek applicants to an **upper-secondary certificate plus results of the relevant entrance examination**.
- UGent: most foreign qualifications get no automatic admission. Each file is assessed individually for equivalence of level and competencies.

### French Community (ULB, UCLouvain, ULiège, UNamur, UMONS, UCLouvain Saint-Louis Bruxelles + hautes écoles)
- **Mandatory equivalence** of the foreign diploma to the CESS, issued by the *Service des équivalences* of the Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles.
- Filing window: **15 November to 15 July** before enrolment.
- The file must prove the certificate gives access to higher education in Uzbekistan.
- **Medicine, dentistry, civil engineering** (and **veterinary from Sept 2027**) also require an **entrance exam**.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age rule and no age ceiling in either community.
- Non-EEA applicants face **early application deadlines** (visa processing).

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 (W 5.5) | Typical English-programme requirement is B2/C1, often IELTS 6.5 overall. Some programmes ask for **6.0 or 6.5 in each band**, so a Writing retake protects you. |
| Uzbek national-test result | **Key evidence** of "access in the home country" (KU Leuven, VUB). |
| SAT | Not formally used. Can support a holistic file. |
| French B2/C1 | Required for virtually all French-Community bachelor's. |

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Category A: Direct entry (Flanders, English-taught, with home-access proof)
- **VUB (Vrije Universiteit Brussel):** English bachelor's including **Business Economics**, Engineering Sciences, Computer Science (verify current EN status), Political Science / Social Sciences, Communication Studies. Non-EEA deadline **31 Mar (2026 cycle)**; language certificate may follow by 30 Jun. €50 fee.
- **KU Leuven:** English bachelor's include **Philosophy** (Institute of Philosophy), **Theology & Religious Studies**, and **Business Administration (Brussels campus)**. Non-EEA deadline about **1 Mar**.
- **UGent:** **Bachelor of Social Sciences** is its English-taught exception. All other bachelor's are in Dutch.
- **Brussels School of Governance** (VUB-affiliated; former Vesalius College): English BA programmes, US-style admission.

### Category B: Score/exam conditional
- Any **Dutch-taught** Flemish programme: Dutch B2 (ITNA/ITACE test).
- English programmes that impose **6.0+ per IELTS band**: you need Writing ≥ 6.0.
- Flemish hogescholen (universities of applied sciences) with English bachelor's (selection varies):
  - **Howest – Digital Arts & Entertainment** (English, Kortrijk)
  - selected English programmes at Thomas More, UCLL, Artevelde, HOGENT, Karel de Grote, AP Hogeschool, Erasmushogeschool Brussel, PXL, VIVES, Odisee

### Category C: Pathway / bridge
- **All French-Community universities and hautes écoles**:

  | Universities | Hautes écoles (examples) |
  |---|---|
  | ULB, UCLouvain, ULiège, UNamur, UMONS, UCLouvain Saint-Louis Bruxelles | HELMo, HEPL, HE Vinci, ICHEC (business), EPHEC, HELHa, Henallux, HEH, HE2B, HELB, HE Galilée, HE Francisco Ferrer |

  Requirements: FWB equivalence + French (B2/C1). If you lack French, a one-year French course + equivalence is the bridge. Medicine/dentistry/civil engineering add an entrance exam.

### Category D: Ineligible
- **Royal Military Academy (KMS/ERM):** Belgian nationality.
- **United International Business Schools (UIBS) and similar private non-community-accredited schools:** not ineligible, but **their degrees are not Belgian state degrees**. Avoid if recognition matters.
