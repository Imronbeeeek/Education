## 1. How Sweden treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- **universityadmissions.se, country instructions (Uzbekistan, bachelor's):** these qualifications **meet the general entry requirements**:
  - *Umumiy O'rta Ta'lim To'g'risida Shahodatnoma* (Certificate of Full Secondary Education)
  - *Akademik Litsey Diplomi*
  - *O'rta Ma'lumot To'g'risida Shahodatnoma*
  - *Kasb-Hunar Kolleji Diplomi*
- Documents: upper-secondary records that **qualify you for higher education in Uzbekistan**, plus passport ID page.
- **Specific entry requirements** (e.g. Mathematics 2/3b/3c/4, Physics 1/2, Chemistry 1) are assessed from your transcript. Your Algebra, Geometry, Physics and Chemistry (all 5/5) give a good chance for business, social science and IT programmes. Engineering programmes requiring **Mathematics 4 / Physics 2** may need a course description showing calculus content ("Algebra and basics of analysis" in grades 10–11). Ask your school for a **syllabus letter**.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age rule and no age ceiling.
- **Residence permit for studies** (Migrationsverket): your passport must be valid for the permit period. Renew it before applying (after admission, Apr–May 2027).

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| **English 6** | IELTS Academic **6.5 overall, no section below 5.5** → **you meet it (W 5.5 = minimum)** ✔ |
| English 5 (some programmes) | IELTS 6.5 / 5.5 → ✔ |
| SAT | **Not used** in the national selection, which ranks international applicants mainly on converted school grades (your 5.0/5.0 converts to the top). Only privately run admissions such as **SSE** use the SAT |
| Retake | Not necessary for eligibility. Useful as a safety margin and for other countries |
