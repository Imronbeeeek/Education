# Norway 🇳🇴

**Overall verdict:** **C**. Norway's official **GSU list** states that for Uzbekistan, *general study competence* requires the **Attestat plus 1 year of higher education** (or Academic Lyceum diploma plus 1 year). It becomes **A** in Scenario S2 (one year of university already completed).
**Fit: ★★ (★★★★ in S2)**
**Confidence:** High (GSU list entry confirmed)

## 1. How Norway treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
**GSU list (HK-dir / formerly NOKUT), Uzbekistan:**
- *"Umumiy O'rta Ta'lim To'g'risida Attestat" **+ 1 year of higher education**,* or
- *"Akademik litsey diplomi" **+ 1 year of university education**.*

Norwegian and English language requirements are listed as **"not covered"**, so they must be documented separately.

This applies to **every** Norwegian university and university college that admits through **Samordna opptak** or locally.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age rule and no age ceiling.
- Non-EU tuition fees were introduced in 2023 (ignored per your brief).

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 | Norway's English requirement for applicants from Uzbekistan is typically **IELTS Academic 6.0 overall** (some institutions 6.5). ✔ |
| SAT | **No effect** on GSU |
| The +1 year of HE | **The only lever.** It must be completed, accredited university study with all credits passed (60 ECTS-equivalent) |
