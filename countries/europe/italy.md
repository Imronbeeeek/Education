# Italy 🇮🇹

**Overall verdict:** **C** at every university (public and private). The Ministry of University and Research (MUR) requires **12 years of schooling**. An 11-year Attestat must be supplemented by **one completed year of university (all exams passed)** or a **Foundation course** that compensates the missing year. **A** in Scenario S2.
**Fit: ★★★ (pathway is well-organised); ★★★★ in S2**
**Confidence:** High (MUR rule confirmed; Bocconi confirms the same rule)

## 1. How Italy treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
The MUR annual circular on international students:
- First-cycle degrees require a school-leaving qualification obtained **after at least 12 years of schooling**.
- If the foreign system has **11 years**, you must add **one** of:
  1. an academic certificate showing **one full year of university** with exams passed;
  2. a **post-secondary non-tertiary** qualification;
  3. a **Foundation course/year** certificate (Italian or foreign) that **fills the missing year**.
- Supporting documents: a **CIMEA Statement of Comparability/Verification**, or a *Dichiarazione di Valore* from the Italian embassy (Tashkent), plus an apostilled Attestat.
- **Bocconi** states the same thing explicitly for 11-year diplomas.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age or age limit for degree enrolment.
- Bocconi: non-residents under 17 on 1 Sep 2027 are ineligible. Not relevant to you.
- **Visa:** pre-enrolment on **Universitaly** is mandatory. The national visa-application deadline for 2027/28 is **31 Oct 2027**, but universities set much earlier internal deadlines (often Mar–Jul).

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| SAT | Bocconi accepts SAT (min **1040 total, 520 per section** as of Aug 2026), ACT, or the online Bocconi test. **It does not cure the 11-year gap**, only selection |
| TOLC / TIL / TOL / IMAT-type tests | Programme-specific entrance tests (Politecnico di Milano TOL, Politecnico di Torino TIL, CISIA TOLC-E/I/S, IMAT for English-taught medicine (check 2027 format)) |
| IELTS 7.5 | English-taught programmes typically need B2 (IELTS 5.5–6.5) → ✔ |

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Category C: pathway required (current profile) — applies to all Italian universities

- **University of Pavia – Foundation Year Program** (Italian-taught + area studies; 2026/27 ran 12 Oct 2026 → 30 Jun 2027)
- **Ca' Foscari Venice – Foundation Year** (contact foundation.cfsie@unive.it)

### US-accredited institutions in Italy (separate case)
- **John Cabot University (Rome)** and **The American University of Rome** award **US degrees**. They follow US admission practice and generally accept 11-year national diplomas directly. → **A** (but the degree is American, not Italian).
