# Portugal 🇵🇹

**Overall verdict:** **A**. Under the **International Student Statute** (*Estatuto do Estudante Internacional*), any qualification that **gives access to higher education in the country where it was issued** qualifies. Institutions then apply their own entrance exams or document review and language conditions.
**Fit: ★★★★**
**Confidence:** High (statute principle); Medium (programme-level details)

## 1. How Portugal treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- International-student contests ("concurso especial de acesso para estudantes internacionais") require:
  - a qualification that **grants the right to apply to and enter higher education in the country of origin**, plus a declaration that it does;
  - completion of at least secondary education or equivalent.
- No mechanical 12-year rule. Each institution sets **specific entrance requirements**: subject exams equivalent to the Portuguese *provas de ingresso*, often institution-run (sometimes online), or document-based assessment.
- **Nova SBE** (English bachelor's in Economics, Management, Finance): **high-school diploma + last 3 years' transcripts + CV + motivation letter + English**. **No SAT required.** Applications go through Nova's **own international call**, not the national DGES contest.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age or age limits.
- Portuguese **student visa (D4)** applied for at the competent Portuguese consulate. VFS / AIMA backlogs can be long, so apply early.

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 | Nova SBE needs **IELTS 6.0** / TOEFL 80 → ✔. Católica similar |
| SAT | Optional; can help competitive programmes (Católica, Nova) |
| Portuguese | Needed for Portuguese-taught programmes (most public offerings), typically B2 |
