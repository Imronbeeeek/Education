# Finland 🇫🇮

**Overall verdict:** **A** at most universities of applied sciences (UAS) and several universities. **B** at the University of Helsinki and Aalto, which require **IELTS Writing 6.0**; Aalto also requires the **SAT**.
**Fit: ★★★★**
**Confidence:** High (eligibility principle and dates); Medium (programme-level score rules)

## 1. How Finland treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Finnish eligibility rule: a qualification that **gives eligibility for higher education in the country where it was completed**. The Uzbek Attestat does, so you are **generally eligible**.
- Finland does **not** count years mechanically.
- Admission itself is by:
  - **certificate-based selection**, mostly for IB/EB/RP/DIA or SAT/ACT holders, and/or
  - **entrance examinations** (university-specific, or the national online **UAS Exam** for universities of applied sciences).

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age rule, no age ceiling.
- **Language-test validity must last through the whole application processing period** (Helsinki rule). Your Sept-2025 IELTS (expires 2 Sep 2027) technically covers a Jan–Jun 2027 process, but the Writing band fails Helsinki/Aalto anyway, so retake.

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Category A: Direct entry with current profile (entrance exam / UAS Exam)
**Universities of Applied Sciences (22)**, nearly all with English bachelor's (Business IT, International Business, Engineering, Nursing, Tourism, etc.) selected via the **UAS Exam** or programme tests:

| Region | UAS |
|---|---|
| Helsinki area | Metropolia, Haaga-Helia, Laurea, Arcada, Diak, Humak |
| Southern / western | Novia, TAMK (Tampere), Turku UAS (Turku AMK), HAMK (Häme), LAB, XAMK (South-Eastern Finland), SAMK (Satakunta), VAMK (Vaasa) |
| Central / eastern | JAMK (Jyväskylä), Savonia, Karelia, SeAMK (Seinäjoki), Centria |
| Northern | KAMK (Kajaani), Lapland UAS, OAMK (Oulu) |

**Universities whose English bachelor's accept Writing 5.5** (verify per programme):
- **Tampere University**: Bachelor's in Science and Engineering
- **LUT University**: Bachelor's in Technology & Engineering Science; BSc Business
- **University of Oulu**, **University of Turku**, **University of Vaasa** (BSc Business Studies), **University of Jyväskylä**, **Åbo Akademi University**, **University of Eastern Finland**, **University of Lapland**: English bachelor's as offered each year

### Category B: Score-conditional
- **University of Helsinki**: English bachelor's (e.g. Bachelor's Programme in Science). Needs **Writing ≥ 6.0** plus an entrance exam.
- **Aalto University**: BSc Science & Technology (e.g. Digital Systems & Design), BSc Economics & Business Administration (International Business, Mikkeli). Needs **SAT** plus Writing 6.0.

### Category C
- None required. Finland is a direct-entry country for you.

### Category D
- **National Defence University** (Finnish citizens).
- **Police University College** (Finnish-language, citizenship).
- **University of the Arts Helsinki**: not barred, but entrance auditions; most bachelor's are taught in Finnish/Swedish.
