# Malta 🇲🇹

> **Update 8 Oct 2026:** the University of Malta's non-EU visa deadline was 1 Jul 2026, 14:00 (later applications move to the next intake). See [`strategy/13-tier3-oct-2026.md`](../../strategy/13-tier3-oct-2026.md).

**Overall verdict:**
- **University of Malta (UM): C.** It requires qualifications comparable to the **Maltese Matriculation Certificate** (an A-level-type, 13-year standard); UM offers **Foundation Studies**.
- **Private / US-style institutions: A.** The American University of Malta and similar.

**Fit: ★★½**
**Confidence:** Medium

## 1. How Malta treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- UM accepts foreign qualifications **"provided the required mix of subjects and levels is comparable to the Matriculation Certificate"** (two Advanced and three Intermediate subjects, plus SEC passes). Comparable examples it cites: **A-levels, IB, US Advanced Placement**.
- Non-European credentials are assessed by **MQRIC** (within the MFHEA). UM then **re-evaluates** them against its own entry requirements.
- An 11-year school-leaving certificate without national exam grades is not comparable to Matriculation. Expect **Foundation Studies** or **≥1 year of university**.
- **MCAST** (public vocational college, MQF levels 1–6): degree-level (MQF 6) entry needs an **MQF 4** qualification (Matsec/A-level equivalent or MCAST Advanced Diploma).

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age or age limit.
- Third-country students need a Maltese study visa / residence permit through Identità. Allow time.

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 (W 5.5) | UM typically wants IELTS 6.0–6.5 with sub-scores for some courses. Check Writing |
| AP exams / A-levels | **The real lever** for direct UM entry (comparable to Matriculation). Not feasible for 2027 (May/June 2027 sittings, results in July) |
| SAT | AUM (US-style) may use it; UM does not |
