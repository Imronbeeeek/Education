# Netherlands 🇳🇱

**Overall verdict:** split by institution type.
- **Universities of Applied Sciences (HBO / hogescholen): A**, where the Attestat is assessed at HAVO level or higher.
- **Research universities (WO): C**. They require VWO-equivalence; CIS 11-year attestats are normally judged below VWO. The usual bridges are a **first year of university, a foundation year, or an HBO propedeuse**.
- **Colloquium Doctum (21+):** you are age-eligible, but the exams are mostly Dutch VWO state exams.

**Fit: ★★★★ (HBO) / ★★½ (WO)**
**Confidence:** Medium. Analogue confirmed: UvA requires Russian Attestat + a completed first university year. Uzbek-specific Nuffic wording was not seen.

## 1. How the Netherlands treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Dutch admission compares foreign diplomas with **VWO** (pre-university, 6 years of secondary, 12 in total), required for **WO**, and **HAVO / MBO-4**, required for **HBO**. Nuffic's country evaluations guide the universities.
- **Analogue (High confidence):**
  - UvA requires, for **Russia**, *"Attestat o srednem obshchem obrazovanii **plus a successfully completed first year of full-time university Bachelor education**."*
  - For **Kazakhstan**, UvA lists the Attestat alone.
  - The Uzbek post-2017 11-year Attestat is structurally like Russia's, so expect the **"+1 year"** rule at most WO programmes. Get the official answer from each university.
- **HBO:** Saxion and others accept non-Dutch diplomas "**at least equivalent to VWO, HAVO or MBO-4**." CIS 11-year attestats generally reach **HAVO-level**, so you are admissible.
- **Tilburg-style route:** complete **one year at a Dutch HBO**, earn the **propedeuse** (first-year certificate), then re-apply to WO.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age rule.
- **Age is an advantage:** at **21+** you qualify for the **Colloquium Doctum** (entrance examination for applicants without the required diploma). However:
  - it usually requires **3–4 Dutch national VWO state exams (in Dutch)**, e.g. Mathematics B for technical studies, plus English;
  - the **non-EU deadline is about 1 Apr**.

  Some faculties (e.g. Maastricht Law, Erasmus) run their own CD formats; ask for an English-language CD.
- **Language-test age:** test results must be **no older than 2 years before the start of the academic year** (Tilburg). Your 02 Sep 2025 IELTS is **2 years and a few days old** at a Sept-2027 start → **likely invalid**. Retake.

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS W 5.5 | Most WO and many HBO programmes require **IELTS 6.0 overall with ≥ 5.5/6.0 per band**; selective programmes (PPLE, University Colleges, Law) want **6.5–7.0 overall, 6.0–6.5 per band**. **Retake Writing to ≥ 6.5** |
| SAT / AP | Some WO programmes accept **AP exams (3–4 subjects, ≥4)** in place of VWO-equivalence for US-type diplomas. Not standard for national diplomas; ask |
| Maths certificate | Selective programmes ask for **Mathematics A/B-level evidence**. Uzbek algebra/geometry 5/5 helps; some require a separate maths test |

### Category B: score/exam conditional
- HBO programmes with **numerus fixus** or **6.0 per IELTS band** (e.g. International Business at some UAS, Hotel Management): Writing retake.
- **Colloquium Doctum** at WO (age 21+ ✔), if an English-language variant exists for your programme.

### Category C: WO research universities (bridge needed)

| Type | Institutions |
|---|---|
| Research universities | University of Amsterdam (UvA), Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (VU), Leiden University, Utrecht University, University of Groningen (RUG), Erasmus University Rotterdam (EUR), Maastricht University (UM), Radboud University, Tilburg University, TU Delft, TU Eindhoven, University of Twente, Wageningen University & Research |
| University colleges (selective, English) | Amsterdam UC, University College Utrecht, Leiden University College, Erasmus UC, UC Maastricht, UC Groningen, UC Tilburg, Campus Fryslân (RUG) |
| Open | Open Universiteit (distance; Dutch) |

**Bridges:**
- (i) 1 year at a university (in Uzbekistan or elsewhere) → apply as a first-year with the +1 year
- (ii) **Holland International Study Centre (Study Group), Amsterdam/Groningen**: one-year foundation in Business & Management or Science & Engineering, with progression to partner institutions (check the current partner list)
- (iii) Dutch HBO propedeuse → WO

### Category D
- **Netherlands Defence Academy (NLDA):** Dutch nationality.
- Dutch-taught programmes without NT2-II (Dutch B2) by the deadline.
