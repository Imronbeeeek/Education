# Germany 🇩🇪

**Overall verdict:** **C** at every state-recognised higher-education institution (public and private). The Attestat alone leads only to a **Studienkolleg / Feststellungsprüfung**. It becomes **A (subject-restricted)** if you have **≥1 year of successful study at an accredited Uzbek university**.
**Fit: ★★★ (English-track foundation routes exist); ★★★★ in Scenario S2 (1 year of university completed)**
**Confidence:** High (KMK/anabin rule for Uzbekistan confirmed)

## 1. How Germany treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
The KMK/anabin classification for Uzbekistan's *Umumiy o'rta ta'lim to'g'risida attestat*:

| Your situation | Access granted |
|---|---|
| **Attestat only** | **Indirect access**: admission to a **Studienkolleg**, then the **Feststellungsprüfung** (assessment exam). Open for **all focus courses** (T, M, W, G, S) at **all** HEIs |
| **Attestat + ≥1 year of successful study** at an accredited Uzbek university (all first-year exams passed) | **Direct, subject-restricted access** to the **same or a related field**, at all HEIs |
| **Attestat + 2 years** of study | Historically broader access. Check uni-assist / the target university |

Studienkolleg focus courses:
- **T** – technology, maths, science
- **M** – medicine, biology
- **W** – economics, social sciences
- **G** – humanities, German studies
- **S** – languages

Public Studienkollegs teach **in German** and require **B1–B2 German** plus an entrance test (*Aufnahmetest*).

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No statutory age limit for degree study and no rule on how old the Attestat may be.
- Some Studienkollegs prioritise recent school leavers when places are scarce. A 4–5-year gap should be explained (CV covering 2022–2027).
- **Visa:** the German Embassy Tashkent has historically had **long appointment queues**. Book the national-visa (student/applicant) appointment the moment you have an admission or Studienkolleg place. The passport must be valid for the whole visa period.

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| SAT | **No legal effect** on German HZB for Uzbek applicants |
| IELTS W 5.5 | Irrelevant to HZB. Matters for English-taught degrees after the bridge (usually IELTS 6.0–6.5, some 6.0 per band) |
| **TestAS** | Used by some pathways, e.g. Constructor University's Foundation progression requires a **TestAS combined standard score ≥ 190** for students on the "qualification pathway" |
| German B1/B2 | Unlocks public Studienkollegs (state-run, low cost) |

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Category A: only in Scenario S2 (≥1 year of Uzbek university completed)
All public universities and universities of applied sciences (HAW/FH), within the related subject area. English-taught bachelor's exist at, for example:
- TU Munich (Management & Technology – partly English; Heilbronn campus programmes)
- Jacobs/Constructor University (all English)
- University of Bonn, University of Mannheim (Business Economics partly), Leuphana Lüneburg, University of Freiburg (Liberal Arts & Sciences), TU Chemnitz, Hochschule Rhein-Waal (many English BSc), Hochschule Hamm-Lippstadt, THD Deggendorf (European Campus Rottal-Inn)
- SRH, IU, Hochschule Fresenius and others

### Category C: bridge required (current profile)
**English-taught bridges (no German needed at the start):**
- **FH Aachen – Freshman Program** (English-only track or English→German track). IELTS 4.5 for FH Aachen and 5.5 for Duisburg-Essen progression → ✔
- **CBS Prep4University (Cologne)**: private, *state-recognised* Studienkolleg with an English track (W & T courses)
- **Constructor University (Bremen) – International Foundation Year** (English; B1 English entry; TestAS ≥ 190 for progression on the qualification pathway)
- **Lancaster University Leipzig** – International Foundation Year (UK-degree campus, so UK rules)
- **Arden University Berlin**, **University of Europe for Applied Sciences (UE)**, **BSBI Berlin**, **GISMA (Potsdam)**: private foundation years leading to their own (often UK-validated) degrees. Check German state recognition of the final degree.

**All state-recognised universities, HAW/FH, art/music colleges** (≈ 420 institutions) fall here by default: for example LMU, TUM, Heidelberg, Humboldt, FU, TU Berlin, RWTH Aachen, KIT, Göttingen, Freiburg, Tübingen, Hamburg, Cologne, Bonn, Münster, Frankfurt, Mannheim, Stuttgart, Dresden, Leipzig, plus every Hochschule / Fachhochschule.

**Private state-recognised universities** with English bachelor's also require HZB, so they too are **C**: Constructor University, WHU, Frankfurt School of Finance & Management, Kühne Logistics University (BSc Business Administration in English), Munich Business School, ISM, EBS, Zeppelin University, Witten/Herdecke, IU International University, SRH, CBS International Business School, Macromedia, Hochschule Fresenius, Berlin International University of Applied Sciences.

### Category D: ineligible
- **Bundeswehr universities** (Munich, Hamburg) and police / public-administration HAW programmes: citizenship / civil-service status.
- **Music/arts programmes with entrance-age caps** at some Musikhochschulen. Check only if relevant.
