# France 🇫🇷

**Overall verdict:** **A** at private business/engineering schools and US-style institutions. **A (language-gated)** at public universities, which run the Licence 1 DAP procedure in French. **D** at Sciences Po (diploma too old), and **probably D** at École Polytechnique's Bachelor (recency).
**Fit: ★★★ (★★★★ for private English-taught bachelor's)**
**Confidence:** High (DAP and Sciences Po rules); Medium (business-school specifics)

## 1. How France treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- France applies the principle that the foreign secondary diploma must **give access to higher education in the issuing country**. There is no mechanical 12-year count.
- **Uzbekistan is an "Études en France" country.** Every application, whether to a public university, a grande école or a private school, and the visa, goes through **Campus France Uzbekistan / the Études en France platform**.
- **Public universities, first-year Licence (L1):** the **DAP "blanche"** procedure.
  - Open **1 Oct 2026**, closes **15 Dec 2026** for Sept-2027 entry.
  - Licences are taught **in French**, so a **TCF-DAP** / DELF B2+ is required. → language-gated.
- **Grandes écoles & private schools:** their own admission (dossier + interview), then an Études en France registration for the visa.

## 2. Gap-year / recency / age — the French "gotchas"

| Institution | Rule | Impact on you |
|---|---|---|
| **Sciences Po** (international undergraduate pathway) | Diploma **no earlier than Oct 2025**. A dispensation is available only for recent graduates who can document every month of a structured gap (work ≥20 h/week, volunteering, language school, military service) **and** have not enrolled in any degree since | **D**: your Attestat is dated June 2022 |
| **École Polytechnique Bachelor** | For students in their final school year or "**recently graduated**" (taking a year off or currently in post-secondary). C1 English, high maths; SAT/ACT used as evidence | **Probably D**: 2022 is not recent. E-mail before applying |
| **Ingénieur Polytechnicien programme** (not bachelor) | Under 25 on 1 Jan of entry year; requires prior university study | N/A |
| Public universities (DAP) | No age or recency rule | ✔ |
| Private business schools | No published age limit. The gap must be explained | ✔ |

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 | Meets most English-taught BBA requirements (typically IELTS 6.0–6.5, some ask 7.0 / C1). Writing 5.5 may fall short where "**no band below 6.0**" applies (e.g. several BBAs). Retake to 6.5 |
| SAT | Optional booster for BBAs (ESSEC, ESCP, EDHEC). Strong signal at Polytechnique (if eligible) |
| TCF-DAP / DELF B2 | Mandatory for public-university L1 (French-taught) |

Requirements: DAP by **15 Dec 2026** + French (TCF-DAP). Without French they become **C** (one-year French course, then DAP for 2028).

### Category B: Score/exam conditional
- English BBAs requiring **IELTS ≥ 6.5 with no band below 6.0**: retake Writing.
- **INSA / UT (Universités de Technologie) / Écoles d'ingénieurs post-bac**: French-taught entrance procedures.

### Category C: Pathway
- **CPGE (classes préparatoires)**: French-taught, built for recent baccalauréat holders, so impractical.
- French-language foundation year (DU FLE at universities, Alliance Française, Campus France programmes), then DAP for 2028.

### Category D: Ineligible
- **Sciences Po**: recency rule (diploma must date from Oct 2025 or later).
- **École Polytechnique Bachelor**: "recently graduated" requirement (likely D; verify).
- **Military academies** (Saint-Cyr, École navale, École de l'air): French nationality.
