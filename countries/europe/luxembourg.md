# Luxembourg 🇱🇺

**Overall verdict:** **D** at the University of Luxembourg for the current profile. The Ministry equivalence requires **≥ 12 consecutive years of study**, and without that equivalence a third-country applicant is not eligible. **C** at best via an extra year (acceptance of a non-school 12th year must be confirmed). Private institutions vary.
**Fit: ★½**
**Confidence:** High (12-year criterion confirmed)

## 1. How Luxembourg treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Every applicant with a diploma from **outside the EU/EEA** must obtain an **equivalence of the secondary diploma** from the **Ministry of Education, Children and Youth** **before** applying to Uni.lu.
- Recognition criteria: the diploma must
  1. attest **successful completion of at least 12 years of consecutive study**, and
  2. give access to higher education in the country of origin.
- Third-country applicants who don't submit the equivalence before the deadline are **not eligible for that cycle**. The procedure takes **at least six weeks**. You must already hold the certificate (you do).
- Your 11-year Attestat fails criterion (1). Whether a university year or foundation year can complete the "12 consecutive years" is **not established**. Ask the Ministry (Service de la reconnaissance des diplômes) in writing.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No age limits. The equivalence is the blocking step.

## 3. Test leverage
- SAT / IELTS: **no effect** on the 12-year equivalence.
- Uni.lu programmes are often bilingual (English/French/German). Language requirements vary by programme.
