# Hungary 🇭🇺

**Overall verdict:** **A**, broadly. Hungarian universities accept a school-leaving certificate that gives university access at home. Corvinus explicitly accepts **11 years where that is the national standard**. Entrance exams and interviews are routine, not barriers.
**Fit: ★★★★½**
**Confidence:** High

## 1. How Hungary treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Requirement: a **secondary certificate that gives access to university in the home country**.
- Example (Corvinus University of Budapest): *"Completion of at least 12 years of schooling (11 years is acceptable where this is the national standard) and final examinations completed in at least 4 subjects."* Transcripts should cover the last 3–4 years. Your Attestat supplement lists all subjects; also ask your school for **grade-by-grade transcripts for grades 8/9–11**.
- Each university runs its own **entrance exam** (e.g. maths test) and/or **motivational interview**. Medicine/dentistry/pharmacy use written tests (biology + chemistry/physics).

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age rule and no age ceiling.
- **Stipendium Hungaricum (SH)** has **no programme-level maximum age**. Applicants must be 18+ by 31 Aug of the intake year. The **sending partner** (Uzbekistan's Ministry of Higher Education, Science & Innovation) **may set its own criteria**: check age and recency in the Uzbek call.
- Uzbekistan's SH quota has been reported at about **170 places**.

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 (W 5.5) | Typical requirement IELTS 5.5–6.0 overall (Corvinus 6.0). **No problem** |
| SAT | Not required. Can strengthen competitive programmes (Corvinus, ELTE CS) |
| Entrance exam | Maths for business/economics/IT/engineering; biology + chemistry/physics for medicine |

**Private:** Budapest Metropolitan University (METU), IBS – International Business School Budapest, Milton Friedman University.

### Category B: Exam conditional
- **Semmelweis University** (Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Health Sciences), and Medicine/Dentistry/Pharmacy at Debrecen, Szeged and Pécs: written biology + chemistry/physics test plus oral exam.
- **Arts universities**: portfolio or audition. Hungarian University of Fine Arts, MOME (Moholy-Nagy University of Art & Design), Liszt Academy of Music (instrument/voice programmes may apply age guidance).

### Category C
- Not needed. Optional **preparatory (foundation) years** exist (e.g. Debrecen, Pécs Medical English prep) if you want a science refresher.

### Category D
- **CEU**: its undergraduate programmes moved to **Vienna** (see Austria).
- Military/police tracks at Ludovika: Hungarian nationality.
