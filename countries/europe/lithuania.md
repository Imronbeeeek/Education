# Lithuania 🇱🇹

**Overall verdict:** **A** at most institutions. **B** where an institution asks for the **Uzbek national centralized entrance-exam results**. VILNIUS TECH lists them explicitly.
**Fit: ★★★★**
**Confidence:** High (VILNIUS TECH and Vilnius University country requirements seen)

## 1. How Lithuania treats an 11-year Uzbek Attestat
- Requirement: **completed secondary education granting access to higher education in the home country** (Vilnius University).
- **VILNIUS TECH** country-specific list for Uzbekistan:
  1. Certificate of General Secondary Education (*Umumiy o'rta ta'lim to'g'risida shahodatnoma*) or *Akademik litsey diplomi*, **and**
  2. **official results of the centralized examination for admission to HEIs in Uzbekistan** (*Kirish imtihoni*, administered by the national testing centre, today the Agency for Assessment of Knowledge and Skills).
- Academic recognition: through **SKVC** or, at Vilnius University, in-house by the International Admissions Office.
- **Legalisation:** apostille. For Uzbek education documents, VILNIUS TECH points to the national testing/assessment agency as the competent apostille authority.

## 2. Gap-year / age
- No certificate-age or age limit.
- Uzbek citizens need a **national D visa / temporary residence permit**. Universities assist via the Migration Department (MIGRIS).

## 3. Test leverage
| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| IELTS 7.5 (W 5.5) | Typical requirement B2 (IELTS 5.5–6.5) → ✔ |
| Uzbek national entrance-exam certificate | **Required at VILNIUS TECH**; strengthens files elsewhere |
| SAT | Not required. Some schools (ISM, KTU) accept it as an alternative academic evidence |
| Internal tests | Motivation interview / maths test at some programmes |

## 4. Institutional mapping

### Category A: Direct entry
**State universities:**
- **Vilnius University (VU)**, including VU Šiauliai Academy and the Kaunas Faculty
- **Kaunas University of Technology (KTU)**
- **Vytautas Magnus University (VMU)**
- **Mykolas Romeris University (MRU)**
- **Klaipėda University**
- **Lithuanian University of Health Sciences (LSMU)**: Medicine/Dentistry/Pharmacy in English with entrance tests → B
- **Lithuanian Sports University**
- **Vilnius Academy of Arts**, **Lithuanian Academy of Music & Theatre** (auditions/portfolio → B)

**Private universities:**
- **ISM University of Management & Economics**: English BBA, Economics, Finance, Data Science
- **LCC International University** (Klaipėda): US-style liberal arts in English
- **SMK University of Applied Social Sciences**
- **Kazimieras Simonavičius University**
- **European Humanities University** (Belarusian university in exile; English/Russian/Belarusian programmes)
- **Vilnius Business College**

**State colleges (kolegijos, professional bachelor's):** Vilnius UAS (Vilniaus kolegija), Kaunas UAS (Kauno kolegija), Klaipėda State UAS, Panevėžys UAS, Utena UAS, Alytus UAS, Šiauliai State UAS, Lithuania Business College.

### Category B: Exam/document conditional
- **VILNIUS TECH** (Vilnius Gediminas Technical University): requires the **Uzbek national entrance-exam results**.
- LSMU Medicine/Dentistry (entrance tests); arts academies (auditions).

### Category C
- Not required.

### Category D
- **General Jonas Žemaitis Military Academy**: Lithuanian nationality.
